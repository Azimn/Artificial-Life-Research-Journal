# Research Programs

The projects form overlapping experimental programs. A project can belong to more than one program.

## Cumulative character continuity (active cross-project program)

Core question: which persistent causal mechanisms make a synthetic character's history influence later grounded recall, relationships, commitments and choices across context and renderer changes?

The [formal program](programs/CHARACTER_CONTINUITY_PROGRAM_V1.md) connects competing external memory, learned recurrent substrate, hybrid and cue-representation explanations. It links [source-grounded findings](programs/CHARACTER_CONTINUITY_EVIDENCE_REGISTER_V1.md) from Pretorius Neural Network / BioCircuit, Pretorius Connectome / FlyWire, Attractomancy, and The Doctor Lives to a [proposed common comparison](programs/CHARACTER_CONTINUITY_COMPARISON_PROTOCOL_V1.md). Other portfolio lines including DUCK, PEMA, developmental individuality, and champion/challenger remain mechanism donors, not automatic participants in the first-stage benchmark.

Primary questions: RQ-001, RQ-002, RQ-003, RQ-004 and RQ-006. No new PRJ record is created merely for the program itself.

## Portable identity and continuity

Core question: what state must exist outside a language model for an artificial individual to remain recognizably continuous across sessions, models, devices, and interruptions?

Representative work: Persona Engine, SoulCore, Open Persona Specification, Wayfarer, Persona Connectome, PersonaConsole, Self-Ledger, actor-tagged memory, relationship models.

Primary question: [RQ-001](research-questions/RQ-001-persistent-artificial-identity.md).

## Subjective cognition

Core question: what changes when the system distinguishes objective state from what the subject can actually perceive, remember, infer, feel, and report?

Representative work: Subjective Character Loop, TinyPersonaEngine, DUCK, epistemic ledgers, first-person rendering, language-lesion tests.

Primary question: [RQ-004](research-questions/RQ-004-subjective-access.md).

## Digital organisms and motivated cognition

Core question: can ongoing needs, motives, affect, concerns, commitments, habits, relationships, memory, planning, action, and learning form a continuously changing subject rather than a sequence of prompted responses?

Representative work: Digital Subject, Creature Runtime, DUCK, MicroPsiDUCK, Gelatinblob.

## Developmental individuality

Core question: can identical founders become different adults because their experienced histories differ?

Representative work: JELLY developmental experiments, Kurzweil Brain Experiments, Pretorius biography development, Accelerated Life, Persona Connectome development.

Primary question: [RQ-002](research-questions/RQ-002-developmental-individuality.md).

## Persistent substrates

Core question: where can persistent organization live so that history changes what the system can become next?

Representative work: JELLY Jar, Native Jar, Pretorius Neural Network, Persona Connectome, Gelatinblob, Latent Organism.

Primary question: [RQ-003](research-questions/RQ-003-persistent-substrates.md).

## Emergent ecology

Core question: can individuality and stable behavior emerge from local information access, scarce processing, resource competition, and repeated interaction rather than from a centralized scripted personality?

Representative work: PEMA, multi-organism PEMA, content-blind allocation, persistent concern reserve.

Primary question: [RQ-005](research-questions/RQ-005-emergent-ecology.md).

## Artificiality and believability

Core question: which causal mechanisms actually reduce recognizable symptoms of artificiality in long-term interaction?

Representative work: artificiality symptom battery, 11-mechanism model, Champion versus Challenger, Integration Harness, Bicentennial Man.

Primary question: [RQ-006](research-questions/RQ-006-artificiality-and-believability.md).

## Bounded adaptation and self-modification

Core question: can an artificial organism change its own mechanisms while fixed external evaluation distinguishes genuine improvement from benchmark exploitation?

Representative work: Bicentennial Man, Surface-Mutable Control, Mechanism-Mutable DUCK, rollback and phylogeny concepts.

Primary question: [RQ-007](research-questions/RQ-007-bounded-self-modification.md).
