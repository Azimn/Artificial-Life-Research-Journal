## Numbering note

The initial IDs are reconstruction IDs, not claims about the historical order in which every experiment occurred. Future experiments should receive IDs when first registered. Historical items can be renumbered only before they acquire external citations.

The 2026 audit intentionally distinguishes recovered experimental designs from executed results. A proposed experiment remains valuable historical evidence, but it should not be described as completed until source artifacts support that claim.
