# Dyadic Constitution Hypothesis: partner-specific continuity infrastructure

**Source status (October 9, 2026):** This is a journal-side reconstruction of the current DCH research question and experimental decisions, **not** an imported copy or approved edition of the DCH v0.2 prospectus. A working v0.1 prospectus and independent revision brief were discussed outside this repository. The revised v0.2 artifact, its full bibliography, revision hash, and line-by-line audit were not present in the journal at the time of this entry. Do not cite this note as DCH v0.2.

## Research question

Can a durable human-AI relationship maintain partner-specific artificial character behavior through a distributed, history-dependent control and memory loop that cannot be explained by the model alone, a static archive alone, or human projection alone? The proposed unit of analysis is the **dyad**, not the language model in isolation. A partner may supply corrective history, characteristic cues, expectations, interpretations and continuity rituals; an agent may hold structured relational state and selectively update future behavior. A working dyad can be behaviorally stable even while its components change. This is an empirical engineering hypothesis, not a claim of conscious co-subjectivity or a metaphysically shared mind.

The narrow DCH claim is that **interaction-specific feedback and memory practices have a measurable causal contribution to later grounded character behavior under controlled history and budget conditions**. The strongest alternative explanation is that an ordinary external memory and feedback controller fully accounts for the effect. If so, the architectural finding can still be useful, but stronger dyadic-constitution language is not warranted.

## Mechanism boundaries

Distinguish the partner's unaided memory, the AI's persistent external record, model priors, the locally active dialogue context, the partner's prompts and corrections, and the evaluation of the resulting performance. A good relationship transcript alone does not identify which component supplies the continuity. Distinguish **behavioral carryover**, **accurate autobiographical and relationship recall**, **value and boundary stability**, and **obligation-sensitive action**. These endpoints must be assessed separately rather than combined into an impressionistic identity score.

The account must also separate real continuity from the observer's interpretation of continuity. Human partner judgments are valuable dependent variables, but independent source-grounded and counterfactual evaluation is required to infer machine-side or coupled-system causal effects. Neither users' investment nor the presence of affectively engaging language proves an enduring artificial subject.

## Registered-design candidates, not completed experiments

**Partner swap:** Hold architecture, archive access, task distribution and intervention budget fixed, then switch an informed long-term partner for a knowledgeable stranger or a differently trained partner. Counterbalance familiarity and instructions. Measure objective partner-specific correction and decision fidelity as well as perceived continuity. If the effect follows the same archive regardless of partner, the strong partner-specific explanation weakens.

**Architecture degradation:** Hold the partner and interaction history fixed while disabling or impairing selected machine-side identity, relationship, consolidation or policy components, with a sham intervention. This tests whether observed continuity relies on agent-side organization rather than solely on a skilled interlocutor who reconstructs the character in every turn.

**Scribe / external-memory ablation:** Remove, swap or corrupt the curated continuity record while retaining partner history and other conditions. Separately compare matched correct-archive and wrong-archive cases. A full-information archive that restores the same behavior without the original partner supports archive sufficiency over an irreducible-dyad interpretation.

Each comparison needs matched conversational exposure, versioned model and retrieval settings, blinded scoring, advance stop rules, partner and scenario counterbalancing, and explicit privacy/consent protections. Longitudinal trajectory audits must test previously unseen commitments and counterfactual decisions, not merely recognition of familiar phrasing. Any future dyad participant logs require consent, minimization and access controls before public archiving.

## Rival explanations and discriminating predictions

The **projection-only** rival predicts elevated partner-reported recognition without corresponding improvement in externally adjudicated behavior. The **archive-sufficiency** rival predicts that versioned correct external records reproduce the outcome when partner identity changes. The **model-prior** rival predicts apparently similar character styles without private-history accuracy. A **distributed-feedback** account predicts a reproducible interaction between durable agent state and partner-specific interventions after these controls. This interaction must be estimated, not presumed from a high-scoring combined condition.

A distinct variable is the partner's *ability* to perform continuity reconstruction; it should be measured rather than treated as a binary identity label. Participant learning, expectancy and reactivity may otherwise confound longitudinal comparisons.

## Relationship to the Character Continuity Program and SCH

The existing [four-arm external-memory versus recurrence protocol](CHARACTER_CONTINUITY_COMPARISON_PROTOCOL_V1.md) measures where machine-side historical information and learning contribute. DCH adds a **partner/interlocutor factor** rather than replacing that study. The [Synthematic Cue Hypothesis review](../literature/2026-10-09-synthematic-cue-hypothesis-review.md) separately studies how a symbol addresses priors, context, or external records. These can be crossed: partner match/mismatch by cue type by archive availability, with information parity and discontinuity audits. The coupling between dyadic relationship scaffolding and cue-based reconstruction remains a proposal.
