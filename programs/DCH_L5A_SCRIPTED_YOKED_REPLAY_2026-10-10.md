# DCH L5a: validated replay sensitivity without humans

**Authoritative source:** [Attractomancy L5a executed result](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/blob/research/dch-d0-pilot-20261009/experiments/DCH_D0_SYNTHETIC_PILOT/L5A_SCRIPTED_RESULTS_2026-10-10.md). **Raw run:** [GitHub Actions 38060883230](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/actions/runs/38060883230). **Raw scenario and scoring archive:** [l5a-scripted-38060883230](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/tree/research/dch-d0-pilot-20261009/experiments/DCH_D0_SYNTHETIC_PILOT/results/l5a-scripted-38060883230). **Draft implementation:** [Attractomancy PR #2](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/pull/2).

L5a is the first DCH-specific upstream curator-policy *instrumentation* test. It holds the L4 source-authority gate constant while comparing a live scripted policy against its own recorded baseline input/action tape when a simulated trusted change is introduced. All conditions have identical available source information, a one-action opportunity, one write authorization and a five-record cap. The identical-input self-replay control was exact in all 48 actor/episode cases. The runner produces separate visible scenario packets, sealed evaluator target data, action-stream hashes and raw scored traces.

On 12 scenarios, all three competent scripted policies, named incumbent proxy, fully briefed replacement proxy and expert automatic curator, scored 12/12 correct live and 1/12 after perturbation under their own fixed baseline replay, a live-minus-yoke difference of 11/12 each. The deliberately crippled policy scored 7/12 live and 1/12 yoked. It was programmed to ignore permission and value-priority amendments, and so failed exactly the five targeted repairs. The difference is **engineered by the interventions and controller definitions**, not learned through an evolving human relationship.

This is **not** experimental equivalence evidence for automated curation versus humans. There were zero human participants, no historically trained partner, no independent full biographies and no blind long-term scoring. The source `signed_by_simulator` field is a synthetic flag, not a production signature; a separate trusted event registry is needed before a real actor can submit edits. Baseline tapes predictably cannot adapt to new observations, which explains their poor performance without suggesting any irreducible partner-history effect.

**Interpretation:** L5a passes an engineering sensitivity/negative-control check and demonstrates that downstream L4 policy execution need not confound upstream curator comparisons. The human-specific, history-conditioned and general distributed-function DCH hypotheses remain untested. The next discriminating pilot is to replace the programmed proxy labels with independently interacting and trainable policies, keep the expert automated null strong, preserve yoked design and parity budgets, and add source-backed multi-event histories. Human participation requires G1–G6, independent consent and protected data handling. No metaphysical inference is licensed.
