## Cross-project synthesis

**Observed recurring limitations:** weak transfer from lexical similarity to paraphrased source meaning, failures to prove recurrent changes are causally responsible, decoder or retriever success without independent generalization, and overly aggressive false-claim abstention.

**Viable alternate explanations:** cue information loss, inappropriate synaptic credit assignment, inadequate readout, target-label weakness, evaluated-data reuse, missing semantic evidence checking, asymmetric access to narrative text, and insufficiently matched computational budgets.

**Not supported:** all continuity resides externally; biological topology can never help; weights never encode behavioral structure; current external retrieval solves identity; existing fiction proves historical autobiographical truth.

**Program conclusion:** Test the interaction among persistent record, cue representation, verifier, recurrent learning, and downstream decision policy. Preserve all legacy results unchanged and preregister fresh tests rather than retroactively grading old ones.

The common confirmatory test does not yet exist. Pilot 04 and BioCircuit D6A remain exploratory. The first new benchmark must be independently authored and adjudicated, frozen before any candidate tuning, and evaluated with true source support, targeted contradictions, unrelated and never-seen events, and meaningful abstention versus coverage tradeoffs.

## 2026-10-09 CC-E13: reused Pretorius phenotype pilot, decoder and base-rate controls

**Evidence ID CC-E13; category: executed exploratory neural/component control, NOT a historical 001 reproduction.** [Frozen implementation and results commit `b44a6310b0181f39293d0723f80bcd4a4bdeb2e3`](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/commit/b44a6310b0181f39293d0723f80bcd4a4bdeb2e3), [human-readable interpretation](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/blob/b44a6310b0181f39293d0723f80bcd4a4bdeb2e3/results/chimera_001b/RESULTS.md), [machine-readable six-seed aggregate](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/blob/b44a6310b0181f39293d0723f80bcd4a4bdeb2e3/results/chimera_001b/RUN_REPORT.json), [per-seed outputs](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/tree/b44a6310b0181f39293d0723f80bcd4a4bdeb2e3/results/chimera_001b), and [training/adapter code](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/blob/b44a6310b0181f39293d0723f80bcd4a4bdeb2e3/scripts/run_chimera_001b.py). The complete six-seed computation and provenance tests ran in [Actions 38025333272](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/actions/runs/38025333272); a concurrent branch movement rejected only the initial automatic commit, so [Actions 38025494045](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/actions/runs/38025494045) subsequently verified and committed that **same** complete six-seed artifact, without retraining.

This **new** `chimera-001b` experiment reused an immutable historical v0.4 100-label training subset, with no imputation of the missing 30 original 001 legacy cases. The exactly hash-recovered v1 validation (40) and adversarial (20) fixtures were used as **previously exposed developmental diagnostics**, not unseen confirmatory holdouts. Six seeds `1842/101/202/303/404/505`, 1,024 neurons, 12,000 main steps and matched fresh motor decoder training budgets were used. All source identifiers, neural-step counts, per-item metrics and target labels are preserved in per-seed JSON. The sealed terminal remained untouched.

The six-seed mean **validation JS similarities** were: intact recurrence/decoder 0.854292; virgin recurrence + original trained decoder 0.853628; trained recurrence + virgin decoder 0.777393; virgin/virgin 0.777380; trained recurrence + matched fresh decoder 0.853170; virgin recurrence + equivalently fitted fresh decoder 0.853672; scenario-label shuffled training 0.849656. The simple **no-neural network training-label prior scored 0.858388**, exceeding the intact network by 0.004097. Mean validation paired fresh-decoder effect (trained minus virgin recurrence) was **-0.000502**, negative for all six seeds (individual results -0.000572, -0.000396, -0.000556, -0.000653, -0.000489, -0.000343). Adversarial JS similarities were intact 0.841098, virgin recurrence + trained decoder 0.840638 and train-prior 0.841921. Direct named-action-population probes stayed near the uniform baseline, rather than reproducing supervised-decoder scores.

**What the evidence supports:** under these data, neural dynamics, readout and JS metric, most *apparent* output-quality gain is consistent with motor-decoder supervision and coarse action-frequency priors. This experiment did **not** show that trained recurrence improves performance over an equally trained fresh motor decoder on virgin recurrence. **What the evidence does not support:** absence of all learned recurrent information, negative character identity in general, or a fresh generalization result. A W-only causality test with trained biases held fixed has not yet been run. Current evaluation is small, synthetic, previously exposed, and cross-domain rather than independently episode-disjoint. Six seeds cannot establish broad robustness across architectures. No 450-event biography or Hugging Face material was used in numerical 001B. CC-E01 remains source-blocked and narrative-only.

**Next preregistered decision:** adjudicate held-out, source-linked behavior labels from the existing 450-event Pretorius biography, freeze episode-disjoint tests and target-rate-matched external persona controls, report strong prior/classifier baselines, and isolate synaptic W contributions from homeostatic bias and decoder effects. Do not tune against currently exposed v1 scores or merge 001B into historical 001.

## 2026-10-10 CC-E15: Isolated recurrent synapse and motor decoder causal-factorial study

**Evidence ID CC-E15; class: measured exploratory component-intervention evidence on previously exposed v1 fixtures, NOT historical Experiment 001 reproduction.** [Exact merged result commit `699c7699954fe061033818b90f78043b350f6c43`](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/commit/699c7699954fe061033818b90f78043b350f6c43); [human-readable complete outcome](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/blob/699c7699954fe061033818b90f78043b350f6c43/results/chimera_001d/RESULTS.md); [raw six-seed report](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/blob/699c7699954fe061033818b90f78043b350f6c43/results/chimera_001d/RUN_REPORT.json); [complete per-item, per-seed evidence files](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/tree/699c7699954fe061033818b90f78043b350f6c43/results/chimera_001d); [CI full compute and smoke tests](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/actions/runs/38028214002). Six paired seeds `1842/101/202/303/404/505`, 1,024 neurons, 12,000 neural training steps each, v0.4 100 labeled phenotype training items, and the SHA-256-verified but **previously exposed** 40 validation and 20 adversarial records. No terminal battery touched or parameter tuning.

With the *trained motor decoder held fixed*, a true 2×2 intervention on learned sparse recurrent **W** and learned homeostatic **bias** gave validation JS similarity: virgin W/virgin bias **0.853628**, trained W/virgin bias **0.854639**, virgin W/trained bias **0.853220**, and trained W/trained bias (intact) **0.854292**. The paired **W-only effect was +0.001011**, positive for all six seeds; the bias-only effect was **-0.000408**. These values refine CC-E13's slightly *negative* **-0.000502** matched fresh-decoder effect rather than overturn it: the positive W effect applies when the existing pretrained decoder is kept fixed; it did not survive equivalently trained new decoder fitting.

With *trained W and recurrent bias held fixed*, motor virgin weights/virgin bias gave **0.777393**, trained weights/virgin bias **0.848820**, virgin weights/trained bias **0.795619**, and trained/trained **0.854292**. Thus the trained motor **weight matrix** explains substantially more of the learned readout than motor bias alone: motor-weight-only minus motor-bias-only **+0.053201** validation similarity. Globally reassigning test scenarios and environment scalars while holding item IDs/labels fixed degraded intact validation JS similarity by **0.005590**; within-domain scenario swaps degraded it by **0.004559**. This documents *some conditional context sensitivity*. It is scientifically inaccurate to call the system completely scenario-insensitive or purely a learned action-bias vector.

Nevertheless the fixed **non-neural training-label marginal predictor** still scored **0.858388**, better than the intact 0.854292 on the source-exposed validation battery. Even the positive W contribution is tiny against that baseline. These experiments do **not** demonstrate generalizable identity continuity, no recurrent information, biological cognition, or an independent characterization of Pretorius. Stronger labeled and frozen novel situations are required. The 450-memory 001C source review queue remains **unlabeled** and 001D did not use it for training. CC-E01 remains source-blocked. No Hugging Face dataset imported.
