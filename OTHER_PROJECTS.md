# Other Projects Registry

This section preserves work that is useful to remember but is not currently part of the core artificial-life research program.

These records use the `OPJ-` namespace so they cannot be confused with `PRJ-` research projects or `EXP-` experiments.

An item can move into the core research journal later, but only when it develops a clear research question, experimental method, or direct evidential role.

## Scope rule

The core journal answers questions about artificial individuality, digital organisms, persistent identity, development, substrate, ecology, believability, and bounded self-modification.

This registry tracks adjacent engineering, creative, teaching, and production work.

A project can be referenced by both areas without being duplicated as evidence. For example, a fine-tuning project may be documented here as model-training work while a specific result relevant to persona persistence is cited from the core experiment ledger.

## Promotion rule

Move an `OPJ` item into the core research registry only when all three are true:

1. it has a distinct research question relevant to the artificial-life program,
2. it has evidence or a falsifiable experimental design,
3. its inclusion improves the scientific history rather than merely broadening the archive.

Until then, it remains searchable here without diluting the lab notebook.
