# Teaching and Curriculum Projects

## OPJ-TEACH-001: Game Design Firm

Capstone and studio-course model that simulates a working game-development firm.

The course uses collaborative teams, external or realistic client constraints, prototypes, testing, production workflows, and final presentations.

Later versions included UEFN and role-based production structures.

## OPJ-TEACH-002: Graphics Training Videos

Long-running instructional video library covering 3D graphics, character rigging, animation, texture mapping, UVs, lighting, rendering, game-engine integration, and related production techniques.

The collection grew from dozens of tutorials into a substantial reusable teaching library.

## OPJ-TEACH-003: Context Streams for AI Class

Fall 2024 applied-AI class archive containing experiments and instructional material involving film, books, generative systems, and AI-assisted creative work.

Repository: `Context_Streams_for_ai_class`.

## OPJ-TEACH-004: UEFN teaching infrastructure

Course and student-project infrastructure for Unreal Editor for Fortnite, including access troubleshooting, implementation guides, project-scoping material, AI-assisted asset workflows, HOPA/MMORPG concepts, and team-production guidance.

## OPJ-TEACH-005: Game Design program and curriculum development

Program-level curriculum work including undergraduate and graduate Game Design pathways, course-shell redesign, retention and advising work, accreditation-related documentation, faculty training, and incorporation of AI and XR into the curriculum.

## OPJ-TEACH-006: 3D Graphics / Animation / XR course sequence

Instructional design using 3ds Max, ZBrush, Maya or Blender, Photoshop, Unreal or Unity, and Spatial.io.

Projects have included VR-viewable rooms, stylized environments, modeling exercises, retopology and baking, optimization, animation, and portfolio-oriented final work.
