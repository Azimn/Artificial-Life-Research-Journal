# Model Training and Fine-Tuning Projects

## OPJ-ML-001: GPT-2 Eminem plus Shakespeare experiment

A 2020 fine-tuning experiment trained GPT-2 on both Eminem lyrics and Shakespeare to observe the hybrid output behavior.

This predates the later persona research and is best preserved as early model-training experimentation.

## OPJ-ML-002: TinyLlama color-to-hex fine-tuning

A 2024 LoRA/SFT experiment mapping natural-language color descriptions to hexadecimal color values.

The project was used to learn the mechanics of small-model fine-tuning, adapter merging, evaluation, and deployment.

## OPJ-ML-003: Zark / Phi-3 Mini character model

Original character Zark, a sentient AI from Planet Saurix, was used as a specific-character fine-tuning experiment.

Thousands of dialogues were generated with larger models and used to fine-tune Phi-3 Mini. A later attempt produced a model that consistently identified itself as Zark and described itself as sentient.

This is recorded as a character-model training result, not as evidence of sentience.

## OPJ-ML-004: Six-week LLM evolution experiment

A cumulative model-development experiment targeting memory, personality, and long-term character continuity.

Methods included cumulative context, naive distillation, and meta-distillation, reaching approximately 100-message retention before later plans for RAG and continual fine-tuning.

## OPJ-ML-005: General local fine-tuning workflow

Broader experimentation with Axolotl, H2O LLM Studio, QLoRA, LoRA, TinyLlama, Llama-family models, and local GPT-2 training.

This category records the tooling and training practice itself.

## Core-journal overlap

Pretorius LoRA datasets, persona-diagnostic training, and renderer-specific character models belong in the core journal when the training is used to answer questions about persistent identity or substrate-independent character continuity.
