# 3D, XR, and Graphics Projects

## OPJ-3D-001: Project Gorgon production work

Freelance game-art work for Eldergame, including monster and enemy modeling and related assets.

Historical production period: approximately 2014 to 2015.

## OPJ-3D-002: Origins of Malu production work

VFX and particle-system production for the MMO _Origins of Malu_ using the BigWorld engine.

The work was presented publicly at PAX Prime in 2012.

## OPJ-3D-003: Doom of Icewind Dale creature models

3D creature-model work for the 2025 _Doom of Icewind Dale_ expansion for _Neverwinter Nights: Enhanced Edition_.

## OPJ-3D-004: Mixamo Scripts for 3ds Max

Utility and workflow scripts related to Mixamo and Autodesk 3ds Max.

Repository: `Mixamo-Scripts-for-3DS-MAx`.

## OPJ-3D-005: Spatial computing and mixed-reality exploration

Game-design exploration around spatial computing, mixed reality, gesture, voice, and eye-driven interaction, including interest in Apple spatial-computing hardware and educational applications.

This is preserved as an XR design line rather than artificial-life research.
