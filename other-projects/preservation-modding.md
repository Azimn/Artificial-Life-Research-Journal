# Preservation and Modding Projects

## OPJ-PRES-001: Living Preservation

Neverwinter Nights asset and modding work examining modding as a form of living preservation.

The broader argument treats mods, community tools, legacy-engine knowledge, replacement assets, and continued creative production as ways of preserving historically significant games and their practices.

## OPJ-PRES-002: Preservation Modding research

MFA and scholarly work on modding as a living archive, with attention to preserving games, communities, creative contributions, workflows, and legacy technical knowledge.

## OPJ-PRES-003: Neverwinter Nights production and creature work

Ongoing asset creation and module-oriented work for _Neverwinter Nights: Enhanced Edition_, including new creature models and released expansion content.

This overlaps 3D production, but is also preserved here because of its connection to long-term game preservation and legacy-engine practice.
