# Game Development and Retro Projects

Retro-game prototype targeting the Atari 7800, including sprite and character-asset work and agent-assisted build experimentation.

Repository: `Dungeon-Crawler-Carl-Atari-7800`.

## OPJ-GAME-004: SushiNights

## OPJ-GAME-005: Links-3DS

Nintendo 3DS and homebrew-oriented prototype.

## OPJ-GAME-006: Word-Cube / Game Boy puzzle prototype

A GBC-era Word-Cube puzzle prototype and related retro-game experimentation using GB Studio and GBDK-2020.

This belongs here as game-development work rather than artificial-life research.

## OPJ-GAME-007: Sword & Sorcery Online: Lindenwood Forest

UEFN-based student and teaching game project exploring how a game-design cohort could build an MMORPG-like experience inside Fortnite infrastructure.

## OPJ-GAME-008: UEFN HOPA engine

A small hidden-object puzzle adventure engine concept for UEFN, aimed at reproducing some of the structure and presentation of Big Fish-style Collector's Edition HOPA games while minimizing the default Fortnite aesthetic.
