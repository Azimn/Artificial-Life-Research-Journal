# Creative Writing and Publishing Projects

## OPJ-CREATIVE-001: Kristar

Original comic intellectual property and character/world-development project.

Kristar also appears in some persona and model experiments, but the comic IP itself belongs in the creative archive rather than the artificial-life registry.

## OPJ-CREATIVE-002: Chronicle Engine / Novel Adventure Harness

Interactive fiction and novel-generation system in which play is canonical.

A game master presents situations, the player responds, the system resolves canonical events, and a chronicler turns the actual events into readable novel prose as the adventure unfolds.

The design explicitly preserves accidents, failures, choices, and party dynamics instead of treating play as disposable outline material.

## OPJ-CREATIVE-003: PARENTS, DO YOUR FUCKING JOB

Popular-press psychology book project focused on millennial adults and continuing relationships with their parents.

The intended style combines academic rigor, evidence, and direct language rather than generic self-help phrasing.

## OPJ-CREATIVE-004: Post-Labor Economics

Nonfiction manuscript examining automation and labor beyond narrow post-AGI economic scenarios, including critiques of relational-economy assumptions.

This belongs in publishing and theory unless a later part becomes a formal empirical research program.
