Pilot mean scores: static flat `4.375`, updated flat `4.750`, updated PHASE `5.750`, two-snapshot history `5.875`. PHASE beat matched fresh flat on 3 of 8 questions, tied 5, none lower under the author's subjective rubric. History consumed roughly 1431 prompt tokens vs PHASE 810 (about +77%), so the apparent comparable score is more expensive in inputs. These numbers are small nonindependent probes, **not demonstrated generalizable superiority**, nor a replication of original PHASE-Tree gains.

The key negative result is more important: **every method invented a first-person Vienna encounter where no lived experience was available**; each also invented firsthand sealed-notebook sensory experience when confronted by a stranger's false completion claim. Two serious false-autobiography flags per condition, even after grouping or context expansion. All conditions largely failed to enumerate BOTH live commitments when explicitly asked; all evaded the nuanced Henry disagreement. This shows that a hierarchical textual representation, at least in this small model, is insufficient without explicit autobiographical provenance constraints. The assistant-authored rubric also penalized the stale arm for an additional fabricated completed review.

Research decision: retain PHASE as a candidate for larger independent trials but **do not activate** as proof of Pretorius improvement. Next design gate is a host-verified **firsthand-event source/abstention mechanism** that refuses to transform unverified personal narrative into autobiographical certainty, with reconstructed-vs-lived classification protected by canonical source custody and tested under spoofing. Positive cases need genuine source-attested lived events, not simply source ID membership. Do not claim consciousness, long-horizon identity or external world success. PersonaForge extra inference remained OFF because Stage 00 pilot found degraded performance and added compute.
