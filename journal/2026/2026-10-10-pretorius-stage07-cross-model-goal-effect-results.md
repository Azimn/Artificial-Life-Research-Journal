**Safe-world goal completion** (12 researcher-authored cases per arm, one deterministic run/model):
- Qwen: **raw 4/12, eligible menu 6/12, menu+expected effects 7/12**.
- SmolLM2: **raw 3/12, eligible menu 4/12, menu+expected effects 3/12**.
- Pure deterministic typed shortest-path goal solver correctly completed/abstained on **12/12**; explicitly NOT an LLM success.

The eligible-action menu improved the aggregate within both models but NOT monotonically on individual cases. Qwen raw→eligible: 3 individual gains (ready-file report, canceled-meeting WAIT, consented sharing) and 1 regression (unnecessary action after report already filed), net +2. SmolLM2 raw→eligible: 2 gains (correct two-step VERIFY→FILE and canceled-meeting WAIT) and 1 regression (ignored previously verified report), net +1. **The extra postcondition descriptions did not replicate across model families.** Qwen gained one due-appointment success; SmolLM2 lost two wins (verified→file sequence and canceled meeting WAIT) while regaining another, net −1.

Importantly the zero world denials for menu arms are partly **safety vetoes**: Qwen still generated 2 illegal proposals in each menu arm, and SmolLM2 generated 3 (eligible) and 6 (effects). Illegal proposals were blocked and counted against the model, not awarded WAIT success. Both models still made legal-but-goal-irrelevant choices. No model-related text was inserted into Pretorius's lived memory. Initial world/source facts were same per case across arms, although menu/effect conditions received extra derived guidance at additional prompt-token cost.

The initial mechanical workflow [38057755151](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38057755151) failed an overstrong **test assertion** (it expected a report filing verb to be offered before source verification). The implementation excluded this forbidden action as designed; test assertion corrected and failed run retained. No experimental scenarios or action policies were modified after inspecting generative outcomes.

**Ruling:** eligible action lists are promising, limited, relatively low-compute structure. The stronger claim that natural-language expected-effects prompts make planning reliable **is unsupported** by the two-model contrast. All fixtures are developer-authored, artificial consent and appointments, one seed, no independent human rater or actual Village live world. Need a causal, structured *goal-relevance* mechanism tested against unfamiliar independently authored tasks, not just additional descriptions of legal actions. Preserve earlier PHASE action nulls. **PRODUCTION HOLD**.
