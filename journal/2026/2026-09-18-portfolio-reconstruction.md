# Portfolio Reconstruction and Journal Founding

## What happened

A reconstruction of the 2026 research portfolio revealed that many experiments had been preserved unevenly across GitHub repositories, working conversations, branches, and remembered project names.

The reconstruction also corrected an earlier taxonomy error. Latent Organism is not the first foundational substrate experiment. JELLY Jar and Native Jar already asked substrate-level questions, while Pretorius Neural Network and Persona Connectome explore neural and topological substrates.

## Key synthesis

A recurring question links a large fraction of the portfolio:

**Where can persistent organization live such that its history changes what it can become next?**

The substrate lineage currently includes:

```text
JELLY Jar
local artificial chemistry and development
        |
        v
Native Jar
native computational resource physics

Pretorius Neural Network
plastic recurrent neural substrate

Persona Connectome
persistent topology plus dynamic activation

Latent Organism
pretrained representational geometry
```

These are not identical architectures. Their scientific value lies partly in providing radically different answers to the same substrate question.

## Broader program

The portfolio appears less like a collection of isolated prototypes when organized as:

```text
specified persona
      |
persistent subject
      |
digital organism
      |
developmental organism
      |
substrate and ecology
      |
bounded adaptation
```

The major recurring themes are portable identity, subjective access, developmental individuality, persistent substrate, information asymmetry, believability, and bounded self-modification.

## Decision

Create a canonical research journal in the repository:

`Azimn/Artificial-Life-Research-Journal`

The journal will contain the intellectual record rather than duplicating project code.

## Journal policy adopted

An experiment is not considered fully documented until the journal records its question, design, result, interpretation, provenance, and what changed afterward.

Negative results and confounds are first-class records.

Stable IDs will be used so records can survive renaming and reorganization.

## Immediate consequence

The initial journal is being seeded from the January through September 2026 reconstruction rather than waiting for a perfect historical archive. Older work can be backfilled incrementally.
