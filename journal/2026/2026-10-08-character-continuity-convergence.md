# 2026-10-08: From separate prototypes to cumulative character continuity research

## Research question

Where does continuity reside causally: external history and its processing loop, learned neural weights, or their interaction?

## Trigger and design decision

An external reviewer, Calibos, noted that decoder-localized persona expression, lexical retrieval outperforming synthetic imprinting, and negative FlyWire topology/plasticity comparisons appeared to converge on an externalized-memory explanation. The interpretation was valuable but too strong: the Pretorius neural network also demonstrated a bounded recurrent-only signal in Experiment 004, while BM25 and the first local NLI verifier remain unreliable for valid, contradiction-sensitive autobiographical recall.

The project owner therefore directed that the ongoing research be treated as one cumulative program instead of several isolated prototypes. The existing Artificial Life Research Journal was confirmed as already the canonical intellectual record. Creating another umbrella repository would duplicate that authority.

## Inspected evidence

[Neural experiment log](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/blob/main/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md), [BioCircuit D6A](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/blob/main/results/biocircuit/BC01_D6A_RESULTS.md), [Connectome Pilot 03](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Connectome/blob/main/results/imprinting/PILOT03_RESULTS.md), [Pilot 04](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Connectome/blob/main/results/imprinting/PILOT04_RESULTS.md), [Pilot 07](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Connectome/blob/main/results/imprinting/PILOT07_RESULTS.md), [FlyWire MB plasticity](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Connectome/blob/main/results/associative/FLYWIRE_MB_PLASTICITY_PILOT01_RESULTS.md), [Attractomancy collection readiness](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/blob/main/docs/COLLECTION_READINESS_2026-10-08.md), and [The Doctor Lives](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/main/README.md).

These are source-repository published reports, not a new independent execution of their algorithms or audited case-level recomputation. Their overlapping test sets preclude treating them as independent replications.

## Interpretation

The recurring observed problem is unsuccessful transfer from cue association to source-grounded meaning and from trainable decoder or readout behavior to demonstrably learned causal action policy. This is consistent with representation loss, credit-assignment weakness, and verification problems. It does not establish exclusivity of the external loop, impossibility of neural learning, or success of external memory.

## Research and product decision

Continue BioCircuit, FlyWire and Attractomancy with specific discriminating next gates, not open-ended tuning. Keep The Doctor Lives as the definitive production brain, subject to its separate release tests. Use the journal for a shared cross-project evidence register, rival hypotheses, common evaluation protocol, historical linkage and explicit continuation/promote/hold decisions. Preserve all existing project-specific raw outputs and versions.

## Remaining limitations and next action

No independent cross-architecture continuity experiment has been performed. The immediate next action is to implement a deterministic common evaluation contract and independently review new source and behavior probes. Only then compare frozen, external, recurrent and hybrid arms on previously unscored material. Preserve null results and unequal-information caveats.
