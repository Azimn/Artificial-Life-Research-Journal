# Noetic Engine: identity as a morphogenetic field — evidence checkpoint

The Noetic Engine research line generalizes the proposed Eidolon work into a renderer-independent cognitive architecture whose field represents a coupled and history-dependent system of affect, intentions, attributed relationships, self-hypotheses, source-bound environmental state, and optional recurrent dynamics. Historical occult and Victorian terms are **emic design metaphors, not evidence of occult physics, simulator access, biological morphogenesis, or AI consciousness**.

**The definitive Pretorius remains in [The Doctor Lives](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives)**. The Noetic work uses an independent research branch; its proposed production adapter is an unapproved RFC, not implemented. The foundational [v0.2 design PR #8](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/pull/8) remains review-pending, as do the stacked results.

## MATRIX-001 — synthetic causal leverage and nulls

[PR #9](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/pull/9) | [result and permanent raw case source](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/blob/exp/noetic-longitudinal-matrix-v01/results/MATRIX_001_RESULTS.md) | [successful CI](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/actions/runs/38025049136).

416 total synthetic trajectories, 13 conditions; 47 tests passed. Removing intentions changed 29/352 matched decisions, developmental field 21/352, synthetic recurrent field 3/352. The Egregore and learned cues changed scores but **zero selected decisions** when ablated. Oracle removal and substitution of a ritual-style sigil with a neutral key were exact score/action nulls. Changing decisions without a ground-truth answer does not establish better identity.

## MATRIX-002 — recovery and learning both failed operational goals

[PR #13](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/pull/13) | [report](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/blob/exp/noetic-matrix-002-recovery/results/MATRIX_002_RESULTS.md) | [CI](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/actions/runs/38025608142).

160 cases / 800 checkpoint-restored branches; 52 tests passed. The full v0.2 field's final/initial shock divergence averaged **1.9929**; all five profiles exceeded 1, so no demonstrated approach to matched baseline. The original perturbation was retained in the affect field and did not truly expire, so this is **not** proof that homeostatic regulation fails after a terminated cause. Opposite signed feedback shifted slow targets and scores but changed **zero** selected held-out actions.

## MATRIX-003 — source-bound expiry achieves field relaxation, not identity validation

[PR #15](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/pull/15) | [report and raw links](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/blob/exp/noetic-matrix-003-conditional-repair/results/MATRIX_003_RESULTS.md) | [first green execution](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/actions/runs/38028225074).

New independent authored fixture: SHA-256 `28f3bea372bf5c2ebc9eb1519e4b4bf2a4d01ec9756c2faaeeb84d8bf621f749`. 96 matched cases / 480 checkpoint-restored continuations; 62 regression tests. Added opt-in, typed, source-linked transient versus persistent context with separately observed resolution and a bounded conditional target regulator.

Mean final/first field divergence after transient context: conditional repair **0.000205628**, matched drive-only **0.001033275**, legacy affect-based representation **1.386140021**. The old method does not encode a matching explicit expiry and **is not a parameter-matched comparator**. In the conditional profile, persistent hazard maintained a field effect (mean final L1 0.464205374) and separately witnessed resolution removed most ongoing pressure (mean final L1 0.001254665). This is a meaningful **software architecture result** about causal-state separation. No correctness, consciousness, model-portability, autobiographical continuity or beneficial human-like identity effect is established.

Developmental targets and action scores again moved with author-scored consequence, but **zero chosen actions flipped** across the revision control. [MATRIX-004](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/issues/16) is explicitly reserved for consequential policy learning and independently judged *justified change*, rather than rewarding field distance or any arbitrary choice flip.

## Egregore semantic integrity remains a blocker

[EGREGORE-001 PR #14](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/pull/14) | [adversarial report](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/blob/exp/noetic-egregore-semantic-audit/results/EGREGORE_001_RESULTS.md) | [CI](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Noetic-Engine/actions/runs/38025842844).

In 15 assistant-authored adversarial examples, source-span, actor and hearsay guards blocked their tested provenance violations. But six verbatim source-backed statements whose meaning contradicted the submitted belief predicate were all admitted under deliberately wrong synthetic reviewer approval; all six changed action scores and two altered selected policy. A truthful quotation is **not** a verified inference of another mind's beliefs. This blocks production Egregore promotion pending independent semantic and authorization review.

## Program decision

Preserve all nulls and confounds. Do not tune public experiment fixtures into supposed confirmations, transplant a new synthetic recurrent substrate to Pretorius, conflate returned field dynamics with recovered personhood, or claim the symbolic names are causal rather than historical design vocabulary. Require independent case judgments, source-matched static/retrieval controllers, correctly scored actions, feedback sign controls, future commitments, and live cross-model renderer tests.

A future paper may report these as fully source-linked engineering measurements, but **not** as experimental proof of morphogenetic identity. This entry is an independent additive cross-project capture proposed for journal review, designed not to interfere with existing daily journal automation.
