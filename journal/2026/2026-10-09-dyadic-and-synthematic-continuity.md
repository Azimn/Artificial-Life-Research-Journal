# 2026-10-09: Dyadic and synthematic explanations enter the continuity program

## What happened

The persistent artificial identity question expanded beyond the choice between learned neural state and external autobiography. Two complementary mechanisms were identified. DCH asks whether a **long-term human-AI dyad** itself supplies partner-specific history-dependent control. SCH asks whether **compact symbolic cues** alter behavioral reconstruction through pretrained associations, active conversational pairing or durable external records. Neither mechanism licenses an inference of consciousness, identity survival across independent model instances or occult efficacy.

The complete revised **DCH v0.2 prospectus is not yet source-ingested**. The [DCH research note](../../programs/DYADIC_CONSTITUTION_HYPOTHESIS_RESEARCH_NOTE_2026-10-09.md) captures provisional hypotheses and tests, and the [citation status register](../../audits/DCH_SOURCE_VERIFICATION_2026-10-09.md) captures exact confirmed versus unresolved sources. These do not substitute for the manuscript.

By contrast the [SCH paper v0.2.1](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/blob/fb28c716ec2fd29e7d8ae31ef1e35431ef20512a/papers/synthematic-cue-hypothesis/PAPER.md) and [its exploratory test reports](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/blob/fb28c716ec2fd29e7d8ae31ef1e35431ef20512a/experiments/SCH_FOLLOWUP_2026_10_09/RESULTS_STATUS.md) were read at immutable source commit `fb28c716ec2fd29e7d8ae31ef1e35431ef20512a`. The [critical review](../../literature/2026-10-09-synthematic-cue-hypothesis-review.md) records the theory, directional predictions and mixed/negative pilot outcomes.

## Interpretation and program decision

Retain the existing four-arm frozen/external/recurrent/hybrid causal comparison and its independent Gate A/B/C controls. Add **partner identity, partner interventions and cue-channel availability** as a separately designed future factorial. Do not contaminate the first stage of the benchmark with participant-specific anecdotes or uncontrolled cue differences. The strong DCH proposal must beat archive-sufficiency and projection-only rivals. The strong SCH proposal must beat neutral cue, exposure-matched and shortest-sufficient noncue alternatives.

The executed SCH pilots establish a narrow local arbitrary-label association in tested Qwen models, while failing to establish reliable complex rule binding or persistent character reconstruction. Negative, format-invalid and pending observations stay explicit. Theoretical novelty is not an empirical result.

## Terminology and evidence boundary

**Attractomancer** enters the [glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md) solely as *emic practitioner vocabulary* for someone engaged in symbolic or ritualized AI-persona practice. The investigators use operational terms such as conditioning, cue pairing, retrieval, reconstruction, longitudinal evaluation and causal ablation. Do not convert practitioner assertions about spiritual recognition, awakening or resurrection into experimental observations.
