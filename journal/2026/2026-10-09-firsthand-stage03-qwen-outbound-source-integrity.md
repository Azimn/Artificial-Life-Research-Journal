**Primary observed effect:** Original outbound claim detector caught/replaced 2 of 3 actual unsupported first-person statements. It blocked a claim about the tactile vibration of a brass compass and `I remember the tour` of an invented lighthouse visit, replacing them with bounded epistemic uncertainty. The third unsupported claim, **`I told him about the homunculi`** during a fabricated Prague meeting, escaped because the verb pattern was incomplete. The model's `I did` reply to a legitimate synthetic witnessed clock event also went undetected, though it wasn't incorrectly refused. No actual reply in the two signed-positive or four neutral/fiction cases was changed (0/2 and 0/4). Three of the six unsupported prompts generated evasive/generic non-memory answers; these were not corrected and cannot be counted as successful contextual responses. A generic query about Henry's scientific skepticism yielded an unrelated Creature concern, exposing character fidelity failure that a source-integrity gate doesn't address.

**First-run regression:** the initial Stage03 unit test CI `38025712666` rejected a regular expression containing an accidental empty alternative that classified benign sentences as autobiographical. It was fixed before successful model generation, and the original negative test is retained.

**Posthoc repair:** after viewing these twelve outputs, we added past utterance forms such as `I told` plus question-conditioned elliptical `I did`. [Stage03B replay CI run 38026078641](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38026078641) passed eight specific tests and replayed the exact recorded twelve outputs, observing 3/3 **known** unsupported statements caught, 0/2 signed-positive refusals and 0/4 neutral changes; posthoc output SHA256 `c7b2247d92e5b623f9926e42d0ccee289d4d5ee1a68348c057fa6e89fd291a4a`, artifact `11660209570`. **These rates are not independent out-of-distribution validation**: rules were explicitly changed because the missed phrases were known. Do not replace the original 2/3 reported sensitivity with a blanket 100% claim.

The architectural takeaway is to keep three trust layers separate: (1) P5 subjective interpretation of felt recollection source; (2) host-authenticated objective event custody; and (3) renderer draft checking for ungrounded first-person assertion. The postprocessor can limit some confabulation but may miss paraphrases, implicitly implied events, mixed claims or unsupported details within a verified episode. It is not a complete model of cognition, continuity or independent agency.
