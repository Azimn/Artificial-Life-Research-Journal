# Eidolon Engine: first shadow construction pilot

An engineering hypothesis arose from the Attractomancy procedure catalog, recurrent self-binding work, and the speculative Ontological Interface Hypothesis: persistent identity might require coordinated neural processes coupling perceptual selection, source-ownership, relational context and maintained intention, rather than persona retrieval alone. The Ontological Interface Hypothesis is a creative heuristic, not empirical evidence about physical reality.

**Architecture:** Eidolon Engine. Janus Gate = event-ownership/confidence training gate; Synthema Lattice = lexical and actor-conjoined associative cue matrix; Noetic Trace = fixed-decay recurrent prospective-action state. Proposed whole architecture is broader; current executable prototype is a narrow *opt-in shadow module*.

**Implementation:** [The Doctor Lives draft PR #36](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/pull/36); [design document](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/docs/EIDOLON_ENGINE_V01.md); [authoritative results](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/PILOT_V01_RESULTS.md); [per-case archival transcription](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/CONSTRUCTION_ASSAY_V01.json).

**Actual results:** [CI run 38022357708](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38022357708) completed 10/10 targeted tests and produced a JSON artifact. Full brain suite [run 38022357753](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38022357753) completed 334/334 tests. In four deliberately algorithm-matched synthetic cases, intact binding reached 4/4 top-ranked instructed actions, no-learning 0/4, association lesion 0/4, and label shuffle 0/4. **Recurrent lesion retained 4/4 top-1** but lowered mean target probability from 0.33723 to 0.12240; therefore a top-1 recurrence-necessity hypothesis is **not supported** in this battery. The feature pathway can activate on distractor text.

**Real Pretorius integration:** In a separate 128-unit test subject, the live recurring Pretorius policy was read without modifying its authoritative state. Shadow probability of the teacher-assigned `create` action changed from production baseline 0.10537 to 0.41606; the learned shadow is a *counterfactual readout* and did not change Pretorius's actual policy. The database digest and recurrent tick were invariant under shadow calls.

**Epistemic boundary:** The training update is learned cue-to-action association, not learned recurrent synaptic organization. Fixed-decay recurrence influences posterior magnitude but has not shown a distinct useful behavioral gain under a strict direct-cue counterfactual. No cross-model, independent semantic, durable personhood, or consciousness finding. Do not promote to the Character Continuity Evidence Register as established superiority.

**Next discriminating experiment:** independent actor- and episode-disjoint prospective-goal benchmark; cue-only, no-learning, matched lexical-statistics, shuffled labels, recurrent lesions, and hidden false-cue traps; full resource accounting; subject-owned causal influence on actual downstream policy selection only through an explicit opt-in migration proposal. Preserve current negative evidence and do not tune the existing 4-case battery into an alleged replication.

**Disposition:** draft PR pending review, not merged; production untouched.

## D3: actual cognitive policy selection on disposable clones

The same research branch now contains a strictly guarded [D3 matched-clone policy-selection protocol](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/docs/EIDOLON_D3_CLONED_POLICY_PROTOCOL.md), [executed result](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/D3_CLONED_POLICY_RESULTS.md), [machine-readable archive](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/D3_MATCHED_CLONE_POLICY_V01.json), and [original four-assay Actions artifact](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38024363702/artifacts/11659136914).

Four cloned 128-neuron Pretorius states began with identical source/graph/neural checkpoints, and every clone received the **same imposed synthetic ten-action distribution** with `challenge=0.120` and `persist=0.110`. A test-only source-verified temporal intervention of `+0.04` to the `persist` score was engineered to flip the decision when a real lived Henry event and a due commitment both passed the rule. The real `PretoriusBrain.think()` selected `challenge` in sham, `persist` in intact, `challenge` with history lesioned, and `challenge` with clock lesioned. The original parent database digest, recurrent neural tick and checkpoint remained unchanged. Research policy monkeypatch was gated to disposable clones and restored after calls.

CI [38024363702](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38024363702) passed 36/36 targeted tests, full brain CI [38024363692](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38024363692) passed 360/360, fresh install and causal-repeatability passed. This is the first **verified downstream policy-selection intervention on a test clone**, but the result is entirely explained by a hand-designed rule and synthetic margin. It is **not** evidence for learned recurrent neural phenotype, more accurate future behavior, subjective selfhood or superiority over existing Pretorius policy. No user-facing or production decision was altered. D3 remains an instrumentation construction step; future E4 neural and independently authored behavioral gates remain unpassed.


## D3-S high-volume adversarial and native-bridge tests

The D3-S research program was expanded beyond the original deliberately imposed near-tie policy, using four actual seeded 128-neuron Pretorius neural action distributions (`11,29,47,83`), twelve adversarial scenario templates per seed, and matched disposable clones. Protocols were committed before execution, and the full artifacts and negative results remain on [The Doctor Lives draft PR #36](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/pull/36).

**v0.1**: [precommitted protocol](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/docs/EIDOLON_D3_STRESS_PROTOCOL_V01.md), [reported results](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/D3_STRESS_RESULTS_V01.md). **192 real cognitive policy selections**, 12/12 positive source/time gates accepted, **0/36** adversarial negatives accepted, and **9/12** positive cases saw a change of actual action tendency. A naive due-only gate would admit **24/36** of those negative scenarios. On *four intended-creative* cases the generic Eidolon intervention selected `create` **0/4** times, instead always shifting to or maintaining `persist`. This reveals an **action-specificity failure** despite source specificity.

**v0.2**: [precommitted native comparator](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/docs/EIDOLON_D3_STRESS_COMPARATOR_V02.md) and [executed results](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/D3_STRESS_NATIVE_COMPARATOR_V02.md). Adds a fifth condition with **the real existing production state-policy bridge enabled**. **240** actual matched-clone policy calls plus separately tested native comparator. Native bridge differed from neural-only sham **36/48** times; Eidolon differed from native bridge **39/48**. Native selected `create` in **1/4** creative-task fixtures, but it was one seed that selected `create` in *all 12 scenarios*, even negatives. Neither system demonstrated task-aware choice.

Reproducibility: [v0.1 original artifact](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38024927388/artifacts/11659977935) and [v0.2 original artifact](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38025172443/artifacts/11659452638) contain row-wise scores and controls; permanent compact 48-case matrices are filed in `results/eidolon/`. [v0.2 targeted CI 38025172443](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38025172443) passed **47/47 tests**; [full brain CI 38025172472](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38025172472) passed **371/371**; fresh-install and causal-repeatability checks passed.

**Research interpretation:** behavioral outcomes and actor/event-disjoint semantic generalization are untested. Four seeds and reused designer-authored scenarios are not independent scientific trials. A deterministic source gate plus fixed `persist` delta explains the intervention; no learned recurrent synaptic benefit or superior artificial identity is demonstrated. The production bridge being categorical-action insensitive in these cases is a further diagnostic, not proof the entire original brain lacks identity. Keep the branch draft. The next E4 milestone is a sealed, independently authored actor/episode-disjoint task-outcome evaluation with strong native controls, learned action selection, recurrent lesions, fresh decoder, virgin substrate transplant and equal evidence/compute budgets.


## E4-A: Native recurrent-versus-decoder audit — correction and substantive null

The first E4-A execution, [run 38025624705](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38025624705), caught a **plasticity scheduling defect**: `step(..., learn=True)` was called only on odd ticks and the neural update requires `tick % 4 == 0`. **All six recurrent matrices remained unchanged** (L2 = 0). That first run is *INVALID for recurrent-learning causality*, notwithstanding green software tests. This was documented explicitly in a [pre-rerun protocol amendment](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/docs/EIDOLON_E4_AMENDMENT_01_PLASTICITY_TICKS.md), and the first artifact is retained.

The corrected, [executed six-seed results](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/E4_NATIVE_RECURRENT_DECODER_RESULTS_V01.md) ([machine-readable six-seed summary](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/E4_NATIVE_SIX_SEED_SUMMARY_V01.json); [original 1,344-row JSON artifact](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38025788297/artifacts/11660305032)) verified **actual native recurrent synaptic changes**: norm 1.418710–1.439002 across six seeds, versus exactly 0 for frozen-recurrent decoder-only. The matched decoder-learning dose was retained. Heldout actors, episodes and action-specific sentence patterns were disjoint from training; labels remain development-authored and are *not* independent task outcomes.

**Negative recurrent-causality result:** after three cue-free steps, trained recurrent + trained decoder, decoder-only, and hybrid decoder with virgin recurrent synapses all scored **24/96 = 25%** correct. No learned recurrent synapse transplant changed a top-1 decision in 96 delayed tests; maximal action-probability discrepancy was approximately `2e-5`. Both hybrid and decoder-only output `challenge` for all sixteen examples in each seed, yielding the exact constant-class baseline on four equally represented labels. Shuffled teacher decoder outputs `create` for all examples and likewise scores 25%. Trained recurrence with a virgin motor decoder scored 16/96, but that is **not** an aligned fresh-decoder transfer experiment.

The corrected [targeted CI 38025788297](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38025788297) passed **55/55 tests**, [full brain CI 38025788229](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38025788229) passed **379/379**, and fresh install plus causal audit repeatability both passed. These green checks establish reproducibility, not brain efficacy.

**Interpretation:** we have shown that native recurrent weights physically change under the repaired training protocol, but their changes do not generate observable action-specific generalization under this shallow synthetic regime. The E4 learned-recurrence benefit gate remains **NOT MET**. Generic reward-modulated Hebbian learning contains no teacher signal specific to the action at recurrent synapses: supervision occurs at the motor readout. New E4-B work should test source-audited, action-conditional synaptic credit assignment, with an independently authored sealed behavioral bank, matched direct/decoder-only baselines, a properly *retrained* fresh decoder, recurrent/decoder transplant lesions and realized future-task correctness. No production merge.

## E4-B: action-conditional Noetic Crucible implementation — causal null and follow-up

The isolated [Noetic recurrent credit learner](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/doctor_lives/eidolon_noetic.py) is implemented on the native Pretorius sparse recurrent W, with a fixed seed-native action-population feedback map and three-factor eligibility. The synthetic outcome grants check actor, event ID, text digest, class and admitted evidence type, **but are offline fixture assertions, not authenticated autobiographical evidence**. Production never imports the module. Protocol [E4-B v0.1](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/docs/EIDOLON_E4B_EXPERIMENT_V01.md) was committed before code.

Initial CI [38028137823](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38028137823) passed unit tests but the standalone artifact entrypoint failed due to Python script import resolution. A subsequent import-only correction was tested at source `f83492612a73427249b20c9302099f69dde725e9`: [targeted CI 38028252618](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38028252618) passed **64/64**, [full brain suite 38028252630](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38028252630) passed **388/388**, fresh-install and causal-audit repeatability passed. [Unabridged 2,304-row JSON](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38028252618/artifacts/11660768811), [versioned compact summary](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/E4B_NOETIC_MEASURED_SUMMARY_V01.json) and [full interpretation](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/E4B_NOETIC_RESULTS_V01.md) preserve results.

**Observed:** W genuinely changed (norm 0.2231–0.2277) in six seeds; frozen decoder-only W did not. Yet **Noetic 24/96 delayed correct, decoder-only 24/96, trained Noetic with virgin recurrent W 24/96, and independently retrained fresh decoder on learned W 24/96**. All trained readers chose `challenge` for every 16-example held-out set, regardless of target; shuffled labels produced constant `create` and likewise 25%. The largest action probability difference after 12 ticks from recurrence lesion was ~0.000278, with zero top-1 changes. The result is a true negative for this learning rule on this **already-inspected developer-authored E4-A benchmark**; it is not evidence about independent cognition or genuine grounded outcomes.

A remaining **recency confound** is that all training quartets had `challenge` in the final position even after class interleaving. A [preregistered E4-B order-crossover protocol](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/docs/EIDOLON_E4B_ORDER_CROSSOVER_PROTOCOL_V01.md) and separate runner now compare four rotated terminal classes with identical native/recurrent-vs-decoder learning doses and known task corpus. Until its CI output is inspected, it has **no evidence status**. None of this justifies activating Eidolon in production Pretorius.


### E4-B order-crossover result: training recency identified

The separate [precommitted order-crossover assay](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/docs/EIDOLON_E4B_ORDER_CROSSOVER_PROTOCOL_V01.md) was executed on six native Pretorius neural seeds with four identical-count training orders, rotating which of `create,persist,cooperate,challenge` was taught last. [Full report](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/E4B_ORDER_CROSSOVER_RESULTS_V01.md) and [measured compact JSON](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/E4B_ORDER_MEASURED_SUMMARY_V01.json) capture the result.

**All 384/384 twelve-tick held-out predictions in Noetic, and all 384/384 predictions in decoder-only, selected the final teacher class rather than adapting to the test episode**. In each of four training orders, the selected action was precisely the final class for every one of 96 held-out items: challenge→challenge, create→create, persist→persist, cooperate→cooperate. Every order scored only 24/96 matching the balanced development labels; changing final teacher alone moved the selected class in both W-learned and fixed-W arms. This demonstrates a **causal training-order/recency confound in the motor learning/readout**, not character-relevant recurrent adaptation.

These are still the previously inspected E4-A developer-authored cues, so they are a valid *mechanistic engineering* explanation of the failure, not an independent generalization result. **The next research priority is a class-balanced, sequence-robust supervised decoder, followed by truly independent task-outcome evaluation; production remains blocked.**


## E4-C: balanced batch readout removes order collapse, but learned recurrence still unhelpful

An [E4-C preregistered development protocol](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/docs/EIDOLON_E4C_BALANCED_DECODER_PROTOCOL_V01.md) and [read-only batch motor research module](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/doctor_lives/eidolon_balanced_decoder.py) replace the sequential last-teacher update with a single fixed centered ridge fit over native Pretorius neural-rate features. E4-B's recurrent W and the original corpus are unchanged, so this is **instrument/decoder repair on previously inspected tasks**, not independent scientific replication.

[Executed results](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/E4C_BALANCED_DECODER_RESULTS_V01.md), [compact measured JSON](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/results/eidolon/E4C_BALANCED_DECODER_SUMMARY_V01.json), and the [original 1,440-row artifact](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/actions/runs/38058009283/artifacts/11671959635) show a real fix to **decoder order invariance** (maximum 4-rotation weight discrepancy `2.78e-17`). The readout now outputs all four task action classes, rather than always whichever class was last presented.

However, held-out six-seed accuracy out of 96 examples was **Noetic-W batch 31 immediate / 25 after 12 blank ticks; virgin-W batch 35 immediate / 23 after 12 ticks; Noetic decoder with virgin-W lesion 31 immediate / 29 after 12 ticks; legacy sequential motor 24 immediate / 24 after 12**. In-sample scores were 131/192 Noetic vs 140/192 virgin. Native task-action representations remain fragile and recurrence has no demonstrated benefit. At 12 ticks, learned W lesion changed 33/96 top-ranked actions while changing any probability by at most 0.000530421; median top-two margin in Noetic batch was 0.000245. These are **near-tie categorical instabilities**, not stable recurrent learned intentions. The new batch readout's target log loss also underperformed the original motor, so do not present its action diversity as generalized accuracy gain.

## E5-P0 laboratory outcomes: persistent world authority and objective controls

Moving beyond E4-C's fragile action-label predictions, we precommitted and implemented an [E5-P0 outcome protocol](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/docs/EIDOLON_E5_OUTCOME_LAB_PROTOCOL_V01.md) and [event-sourced research laboratory](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/research/eidolon-engine-v01/doctor_lives/eidolon_outcome_lab.py). The world contains named rooms, ordinary persistent objects, repair state, multi-page books and bookmarks, commitments, and a strict first-person attribution gate. Its object truth is separate from Pretorius's subjective claims; actions are persisted in a SHA-256 chained ledger and replayed from the initial fixture on reload. No imaginary fine-grained metric geometry is assumed. This is a bounded simulator *instrument*, not a comprehensive realistic room, not a native cognitive architecture migration.
