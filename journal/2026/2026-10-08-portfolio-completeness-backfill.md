# 2026-10-08: Portfolio-wide repository and experiment backfill

## Question

Which actual experiments and software lines have advanced beyond the September historical reconstruction without appearing in the Artificial Life Research Journal, and how can they be integrated without confusing product work, observational subjects, proposed experiments, and independent causal findings?

## Evidence acquisition

The connected Azimn account returned 56 repos, of which 53 are public and three private. The public names were mapped to existing core programs, missing research programs, noncore game/tooling work, external source code and unresolved lead records. GitHub READMEs, selected source reports, qualified donor tables, source histories and prior journal audits were consulted. This was not an exhaustive reread of every branch, GitHub Actions output, historical conversation or private archive.

The resulting [public repository inventory](../../audits/PUBLIC_REPOSITORY_INVENTORY_2026-10-08.csv), [full audit](../../audits/PORTFOLIO_COMPLETENESS_AUDIT_2026-10-08.md), [research asset ownership map](../../audits/RESEARCH_ASSETS_2026-10-08.md), and [verification queue](../../audits/PORTFOLIO_FOLLOWUP_QUEUE_2026-10-08.md) define the scope and disclose limitations.

## Project decision

Seven new core research lineages were indexed as PRJ-037 through PRJ-043: Frankenstein/Bride, FlyWire Connectome, Attractomancy, The Doctor Lives, Calibos Mind, Agent Pretorius and activation-space affect steering. BioCircuit remains within PRJ-002 Pretorius Neural Network and the current Kiki Mind work remains within PRJ-027. Historical PersonaConsole, Omnicore and legacy cartridges remain within PRJ-009/PRJ-023. Game worlds and local-agent shells are recorded as noncore infrastructure with explicit research-testbed cross-references.

The central [experiment ledger](../../EXPERIMENT_LEDGER.md) now adds EXP-2026-034 through EXP-2026-060, mapping the October findings, nulls, engineering gates, open questions and proposed comparisons to authoritative source files. None of these indices represents a newly executed experiment during the journal backfill.

## Important scientific correction

Decoder-localized phenotype, cue-representation improvement, failed semantic transfer, lack of isolated FlyWire plasticity gain and near-reject-all source checking are related failure patterns but do not establish that all identity must live in external records. Recurrent-only phenotype signals were also measured under bounded altered plasticity. The program therefore retains external, recurrent, hybrid and cue-representation hypotheses as competing testable explanations.

Bride's qualified donor experiment adds an orthogonal result: mechanisms can have local behavior gains yet be refused for redundancy, excessive cost, complexity or authority risk. A rejected donor for one frozen runtime target need not be rejected as a standalone research topic.

## Decision and remaining action

The pre-existing journal remains central intellectual authority; code repositories continue to own original case-level result, data and implementation files. Private archives and thought streams are not mirrored into the public journal. Separate reviewed benchmark and deterministic harness work remain tracked as [Gate A](https://github.com/Azimn/Artificial-Life-Research-Journal/issues/1) and [Gate B](https://github.com/Azimn/Artificial-Life-Research-Journal/issues/2), with [Gate C](https://github.com/Azimn/Artificial-Life-Research-Journal/issues/3) blocked on both.

The audit is a major coverage repair, not a claim of perfect historical completeness. The unresolved source and branch verification work remains explicitly tracked in the October follow-up queue.
