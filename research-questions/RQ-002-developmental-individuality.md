# Developmental Individuality

## Question

Can initially identical or tightly matched artificial founders become systematically different adults because their experienced histories differ?

## Why it matters

A persistent character can store memories without being developmentally changed by them. This question asks for a stronger property: past experience alters the system that encounters future experience.

## Evidence so far

JELLY developmental experiments produced anatomical divergence under different histories.

Kurzweil Brain Experiments produced systematic adult behavioral differences between paired developmental environments.

Accelerated Life applies the same-origin divergence question to a more complex persistent cognitive organism.

Pretorius biography development asks whether developmental exposure can embed phenotype in recurrent neural dynamics.

## Important negative evidence

The Kurzweil computer-access ablation outperformed the canonical condition in all recorded paired lives. This argues against a simplistic reconstruction story and exposed overgeneralized reinforcement of broad action tendencies.

Pretorius Biography Surgery showed that developmental training can still localize much of its measurable effect in an output decoder if the architecture permits it.

## Unresolved tests

The strongest future evidence would combine identical founders, controlled developmental histories, matched opportunity and reward structure, held-out adult evaluation, destructive localization tests, and replication across seeds.
