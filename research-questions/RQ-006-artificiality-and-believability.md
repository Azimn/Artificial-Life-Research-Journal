# Artificiality and Believability

## Question

Which persistent causal mechanisms actually reduce the behaviors that make a digital character recognizably artificial over long interaction?

## Known symptom classes

Current candidates include shallow self-narrative, excessively clean introspection, weak spontaneous thought, formulaic relationship development, insufficient long-term consequences, poor prospective memory, repetitive cognitive rhythms, weak motive conflict, absent habit formation, inadequate models of other people, and too little unconscious or unattended carryover.

## Experimental response

Champion versus Challenger treats existing non-LLM mechanisms as candidates that must earn inclusion through behavioral comparison.

Integration Harness adds provenance, deterministic testing, adversarial review, and ablation discipline.

Bicentennial Man asks whether bounded modification can reduce artificiality while fixed evaluation boundaries prevent surface-level exploitation.

## Open problem

Believability can be improved cosmetically by a stronger renderer. The program therefore needs evaluations that distinguish expressive polish from deeper causal continuity.
