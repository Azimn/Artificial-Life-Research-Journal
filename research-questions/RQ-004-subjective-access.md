# Subjective Access

## Question

What changes when an artificial subject can access only what it perceives, remembers, infers, feels, and notices rather than the implementation's complete state?

## Working principle

The machinery may know numbers. The subject does not.

Objective world state, implementation telemetry, retrieval scores, trust floats, hidden needs, evaluator data, and private identifiers should not silently become first-person knowledge.

## Evidence and implementations

Subjective Character Loop converts hidden numerical variables into bounded first-person awareness.

TinyPersonaEngine separates world facts, perception, belief, subjective experience, and private completion.

DUCK extends the principle across memory, motivation, commitments, planning, relationships, and action.

Language-lesion tests ask whether cognition remains coherent without continuously narrating itself.

## Open problem

A first-person interface can itself become an overly clean narrator. Future work must preserve uncertainty, partial awareness, spontaneous recruitment, conflicting motives, forgetting, and delayed consequences without simply hiding numbers behind polished prose.
