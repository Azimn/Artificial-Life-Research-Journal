# Historical Backfill Audit 2026

This note records a broad audit of prior 2026 conversations against the initial Artificial Life Research Journal.

The audit deliberately distinguishes user-developed projects from predecessor names, absorbed experimental branches, conceptual proposals, and outside prior art. The goal is preservation, not inflation of the project count.

## Recovered branches that should not become separate top-level projects

Creature Lab v0.1 and v0.2 were recovered as real experiments, but they are best preserved under PRJ-019, PEMA-Creaturez. Creature Lab tested long-history path dependence and later bounded ontogenetic plasticity before the PEMA-Creaturez repository became the canonical home.

Platform Penalty was a real DUCK and persona experiment rather than a new project. It compared reusable shared substrate, sparse generic organization, and specialized organization across several personas and held-out cases. It belongs under DUCK and the persistent-identity questions.

Living Entity and Persona Organism were substantial implementation phases, but the surviving evidence places them inside the Persona Engine lineage rather than as independent programs. Living Entity 0.9.0 RC1 integrated persistent identity, projects, off-camera activity, provenance-aware memory, World Authority, subjective beliefs, relationships, rollback, heartbeat, and local or hosted renderers.

PersonaConsole, Pretorius V6, PythonX, P3 cartridges, and related cartridge names represent important implementation generations within the portable-persona lineage. They should be recorded inside PRJ-009 and PRJ-023 rather than multiplied into independent project IDs.

Pretorius Developmental Reconstruction and the Westworld-style digital-clone experiment were recovered as experimental proposals linking biography, developmental attractors, equifinality, and multifinality. They belong as experiments connecting PRJ-002, PRJ-006, and PRJ-022 rather than as a new project.

The anti-artificiality longitudinal lab and minimal-lifelikeness ablation work are methodologically real, but they are better treated as precursors or branches of PRJ-013, PRJ-018, and PRJ-005.

The AIRI Pretorius vessel was an embodiment and frontend integration experiment for a persistent persona. It is documented as part of the persona-runtime lineage rather than promoted to a separate project.

## Conceptual lines preserved but not promoted to project records

The audit recovered Embodied Evidence Engine, Operational Interiority, Meaning as Metabolic Pressure, compression-first intelligence, cognitive phase transitions, synthetic curiosity, error signatures of intelligence, multi-agent identity formation, and alignment as a dynamical system.

These are substantial research ideas, but the recovered evidence does not show all of them becoming independent experimental programs. They remain appropriate for PRJ-020 and future concept notes.

## Outside prior art intentionally excluded from the project registry

Hexis, Shikigami Protocol, Project Alicization, OpenHer, Synthetic Heart, Concordia, OpenLife, PEPA, Generative Agents, PersonaTree, TraceMem, ZifaMem, Project N.E.K.O., MeuxCompanion, Spindl, Agent Bud-E, OpenCrayFish, Engram, JPAF, CiF-CK, and related systems were studied as donors, comparisons, or novelty pressure. They are not user-owned projects and should eventually live in literature or prior-art notes rather than the project registry.

## Audit conclusion

The first journal pass substantially underrepresented the first half of 2026. The largest gap was the portable-identity lineage that led from Pooka, Digital Twin, SoulCore, Project Persona, OmniPersona, P3, MINA, PersonaConsole, and ACOR toward Persona Engine, Wayfarer, DUCK, and the later organism research.

The second important gap was the exploratory architecture line represented by Sidecar Self-Core, CIP, Fringe Mind Lab, and MADMAN. These projects are scientifically useful because they show that the later work did not emerge from one linear architecture. Multiple competing hypotheses about where continuity and individuality might live were being tested in parallel.

This audit is reconstruction evidence. Where exact commits, dates, or source artifacts are later recovered, project pages should be tightened rather than silently rewriting the historical uncertainty.
