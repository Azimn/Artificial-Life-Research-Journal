## Critical missing artifacts and safeguards

The proposed cross-architecture independent reviewer challenge has not been authored, adjudicated and frozen. Connectome Pilot 04 and BioCircuit D6A test questions cannot become fresh confirmatory holdouts. A future comparison must record the exact set of source texts each arm may see, task-aligned versioned feature fitting, and fixed model/checkpoint and storage budgets.

Archived CI outputs can expire; source repositories should permanently preserve per-case JSON and workflow SHA where possible. The journal should point to those case records rather than copying an abbreviated percentage and claiming independent validation.

Do not upload or mirror private Calibos cognition or other protected personal data to this public artifact index. Any future private subject-evaluation design requires a public-safe output schema and explicit authorization.
