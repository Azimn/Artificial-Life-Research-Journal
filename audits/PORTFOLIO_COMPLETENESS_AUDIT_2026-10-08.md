# Portfolio Completeness Audit, 2026-10-08

## Audit objective and scope

Reconcile the September-centered Artificial Life Research Journal with the research portfolio visible on the connected Azimn GitHub account on October 8, 2026, and recover experimental findings that lived only in newer repositories. This is a **repository coverage and source-document audit**, not a complete byte-level reproduction or a proof that every historical conversation, disconnected service, personal disk, unpublished experiment or unlisted organization repository has been found.

The connected account returned **56 Azimn repositories**, comprising **53 public and three private**. The [public repository inventory](PUBLIC_REPOSITORY_INVENTORY_2026-10-08.csv) records the name, branch, public URL, research category and journal action for every public result. Names and contents from private repositories are not copied into this public research journal; the private count is included only to explain coverage. Accessing a repository under the user's account does not establish that its code was written by this author: upstream forks and reference checkouts must stay outside the authored-research evidence ledger.

The earlier [September 2026 historical backfill](../HISTORICAL_BACKFILL_AUDIT_2026.md) and [pre-2026 history audit](../PRE_2026_HISTORY_AUDIT.md) remain preserved. They reconstructed Pooka, the 2023 modular/6DE work, 2024 companion memory, 2025 portable SoulFile and SNP prototypes, and the major early-2026 substrate and identity lines. This audit does not rewrite those records or claim to have newly discovered them.

No cross-architecture causal comparison has been executed in the act of backfilling.

## Significant new negative evidence and its interpretation

Pretorius Neural Network Experiment 001 remains decoder-localized at default plasticity, while Experiment 004 supplies a modest recurrent-only signal under changed plasticity. The BioCircuit source-decision sequence further shows that even trained output decoding did not reliably generalize to withheld labels in D6A, and recurrent W-lesion controls did not demonstrate source-specific behavioral benefits.

Pretorius Connectome Pilot 03's lexical improvement (77.9% top-1 on word-swapped cues) did not transfer to novel semantic paraphrases in Pilot 04 (zero correctly accepted under the tested synthetic encoders). Pilot 05A exposed BM25 contradiction false acceptance; Pilot 07A's local NLI reduced false acceptance mainly by rejecting legitimate source questions. Whole-brain and mushroom-body FlyWire comparisons did not establish a biological-connectivity-specific autobiographical advantage; the v2 MB plasticity report showed no isolated added recall from learned versus frozen original connectivity.

Bride provides a valuable distinct selection experiment: not every effect worth testing deserves production promotion. It retains documented redundancy rejections, cost and complexity holds, and infrastructure-only donors rather than declaring all plausible mechanisms improvements. These results may be local to Bride's frozen target and cannot replace new independent generalization.

## Privacy and attribution

Do not copy any private repository content, private thought data, personal relationship evidence, inbox records, credentials or encrypted backup payload into this public journal. Summaries of publicly released Calibos or Kiki architecture are allowed; private cognition and real human identity data are not. An AI persona's first-person diary is not independent evidence of consciousness or autobiography. Separate authored fictional Pretorius reconstruction from lived runtime events and from documented media canon.

Upstream forks and third-party references belong in prior art, even if locally modified; absent provenance, mark author attribution unresolved. Tooling and game code enter the core scientific ledger only through a traceable, executed or predeclared experiment.

## Completeness assessment

**Completed by this audit:** public Azimn repository enumeration and triage, seven formerly missing core project records, expansion of the experiment ledger with 27 source-linked existing/proposed entries, a cross-project source/data-asset boundary, and linked program-level evidence interpretation.

**Not completed by this audit:** exhaustive line-by-line history across all branches/PRs/Actions, independent recomputation of original measurements, recovery of all material from offline local machines and older conversations, independent human adjudication of existing authored prompts, or private repository documentation that would be unsafe to publish here. An unseen additional repository or experiment cannot be ruled out. Existing publications and the 2023 through early-2026 reconstruction remain linked but were not re-audited bibliographically.

## Rules for future updates

A new public repository is inventoried immediately, but receives a fresh PRJ ID only for a materially distinct research question. Each executed experiment belongs to the original project's authoritative result path and is indexed here with source commit/protocol/case-level evidence; the central journal records question, controls, results, confounds and next decision. When the evidence does not show a run, label it a design or candidate. When a previous claim is corrected, preserve original language as historical and append a dated correction. Make negative results searchable.

The next work is not another wholesale portfolio guess. Maintain this audit against GitHub inventory and prioritize the explicit [follow-up queue](PORTFOLIO_FOLLOWUP_QUEUE_2026-10-08.md), while Gates [A](https://github.com/Azimn/Artificial-Life-Research-Journal/issues/1), [B](https://github.com/Azimn/Artificial-Life-Research-Journal/issues/2), and [C](https://github.com/Azimn/Artificial-Life-Research-Journal/issues/3) mature into an executable common character-continuity evaluation.
