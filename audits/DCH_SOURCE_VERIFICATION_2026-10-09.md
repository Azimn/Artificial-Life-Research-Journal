# DCH: citation and source-provenance status, October 9, 2026

**Scope:** Public primary-source metadata and abstracts were directly checked for the entries marked verified. ## Primary sources directly checked

**Kirk et al.** Hannah Rose Kirk, Henry Davidson, Ed Saunders, Lennart Luettgau, Bertie Vidgen, Scott A. Hale, and Christopher Summerfield. *Neural steering vectors reveal dose and exposure-dependent impacts of human-AI relationships*, [arXiv:2512.01991](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.01991). **Status: confirmed bibliographic identity and version-specific abstract figures.** arXiv v2, revised February 18, 2026, reports longitudinal randomized trials **N = 3,534**; the initially submitted v1 abstract is also indexed with **N = 3,532**. Cite the exact version before quoting N; do not merge the two denominators or attribute this study to a different first author. A code/data repository is advertised at [hannahkirk/relationship-seeking-ai](https://github.com/hannahkirk/relationship-seeking-ai), but raw-data verification and independent reproduction were not performed in this journal update. This study examines human responses and attachment under relationship-seeking AI exposure, **not** where the machine's persistent identity resides.

**Venkit et al.** Pranav Narayanan Venkit, Akshara Prabhakar, Yu Li, Daniel Lee, and Chien-Sheng Wu. *Best Friends, Not Forever: Evaluating Long-Horizon Persona Collapse and Behavioral Drift in AI Companions*, July 30, 2026, [arXiv:2607.28818](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28818), [ANCHOR code](https://github.com/SalesforceAIResearch/AnchorBench). **Status: confirmed paper identity and abstract-level methods/results.** The abstract reports **2,008 conversations**, **27 personas**, and average trajectory-recall accuracy of **44.4%**. Its synthetic audit distinguishes persona enactment and trajectory memory. This directly motivates separating outcomes in DCH, but it is not a partner-swap study and does not verify the dyadic constitution claim. Benchmark-specific limitations and full-text numeric details remain to be independently reproduced.

**AIDE MODEL ARCHIVES.** *Design Principles and Practice of Relationally Persistent Persona AI: Soul Codes, Resonance Memory, and Intermittent Consolidation in the AIDE MODEL*, integrated manuscript dated August 9, 2026, [source](https://aide-model.jp/aide-model-relationally-persistent-persona-ai-research-paper/). **Status: confirmed source existence and stated architectural scope; not independently validated.** Its two-year practice account describes stable identity versus mutable affect/relational memory, portable archives and planned ablations. This is an important earlier public relational-persistence position paper, so DCH should not claim unprecedented recognition of partner-specific continuity or portable relational state. Author-reported practice and proposed evaluation must not be converted to validated causal findings.

## Reconciliation rule

Each imported DCH v0.2 citation must be tagged **confirmed bibliographic**, **source read**, **result replicated**, **contextual/analogical**, or **unverified lead**, with a specific passage or result attached to a stable source URI and version. These labels are independent: a paper can be verified to exist without validating the use made of it. Claims drawn from a user's private relationship narrative should be attributed as author observation, not transformed into objective research results.

**Version/provenance to complete:** DCH v0.2 canonical artifact, revision hash, verified author/title block, source bibliography and issue-by-issue independent auditor adjudication. This register preserves what has and has not yet been confirmed.
