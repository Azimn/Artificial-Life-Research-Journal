# Artificial Individuality Research Lineage, 2023 to October 2026

This is a conceptual and implementation lineage, **not a claim that one codebase literally descends from another** unless a source repository documents the import, donor qualification or shared artifact. Crossovers are labeled by their strongest supported relationship. The published [historical audits](../PRE_2026_HISTORY_AUDIT.md) and [Bride donor history](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Bride-of-Frankenstein/blob/main/README.md) provide the broad ancestry; current code ownership remains in separate repos.

## Evolution of the question

The research changes from "Can a model convincingly express a persona?" to "What durable causal organization and experiential evidence are necessary for an individual to retain meaningful history?" This development did not occur along one linear architecture. There are parallel and sometimes contradictory hypotheses about whether identity is best represented by explicit external state, developmental neural dynamics, event-sourced reconstruction, first-person homeostasis, social interaction or prompt-level conditioning.

## Mechanism transfer map

[DUCK planning](https://github.com/Azimn/DUCK) was qualified for a bounded planning mechanism in Bride, without granting planning a second action executor. [Jelly-Psiduck](https://github.com/Azimn/Jelly-Psiduck) first-person cognition informed bounded inner-ear feedback. [TinyPersonaEngine](https://github.com/Azimn/TinyPersonaEngine) supplied a host-side first-person perception boundary. The Doctor Lives contributed a confidentiality-specific policy bridge, while its generic bridge was redundant in the frozen Frankenstein comparison. Kiki contributed exact evidence/context provenance methods; its personal memories do not transfer into Pretorius.

A separate **data** rather than **neural-weight** transfer connects Pretorius Connectome's canonical reconstructed 450-event source and deterministic lexical cache to BioCircuit's architecture-specific neural input adapter. This is reusable scientific preparation without claiming identical neural representation or learning mechanisms.

Calibos may be a source of public structural mechanism and review insight, but the definitive Pretorius program prohibits copying Calibos's private thought, autobiographical events, relationships or persona cartridge. The Doctor Lives preserves renderer neutrality while retaining a recurrent policy and independent source provenance.

Attractomancy is an intentionally independent comparison at the conditioning/invocation level. Its evidence may later explain stable behavioral reconstruction but must not be used to retroactively claim success for neural experiments. Activation steering is an independent model-specific expression-control route and requires its own evidence and comparators.

## Cross-cutting lessons supported by existing reports

The recurring bottlenecks include cue-representation loss across lexical to semantic transfer, failure to demonstrate learned recurrent causal value over matched lesions, and failure to distinguish a decoder or retriever that matches known inputs from one that generalizes. At the same time, the recurrent-only Pretorius signal prevents asserting weights are inherently irrelevant.

The repeated engineering successes involve provenance authority, deterministic replay, event-sourced reconstruction, explicit world-versus-belief boundaries, and preserving nulls or rejected donors. These successes make controlled testing feasible but are not a substitute for it.

## Current common decision

The [Character Continuity Program](../programs/CHARACTER_CONTINUITY_PROGRAM_V1.md) and [four-condition shared study](../programs/CHARACTER_CONTINUITY_COMPARISON_PROTOCOL_V1.md) are now the cross-project scientific backbone. Individual experiments continue where they have a discriminating hypothesis. Production Pretorius and game-world software progress under their own release criteria. The central journal preserves what was tried, what failed, what worked, and what the result changed.

## Historical uncertainty

Missing unpublished files, inaccessible local runs, alternative repo organizations, obsolete or private branches, and previously deleted projects remain possible. This map is not an exhaustive software-dependency graph. New lineage edges require source evidence; similarity of design terms alone does not prove transfer.
