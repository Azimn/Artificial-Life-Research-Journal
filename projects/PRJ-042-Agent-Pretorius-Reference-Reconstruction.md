# Agent Pretorius: Hermes Reconstruction and Longitudinal Reference

## Research question

What level of autobiographical and behavioral fidelity can be achieved when a practically unconstrained reference Pretorius receives persistent memory, relationships, a revisable self-model, scheduled activity, tools, research-library context, and a replaceable language-model renderer?

## System and distinction

[Agent Pretorius](https://github.com/Azimn/Agent-Pretorius) is a Hermes-based character and research collaborator, deliberately different from the constrained recurrent subject in Pretorius Neural Network and the definitive integrated production identity in The Doctor Lives.

Its public README describes a SQLite autobiographical record, structured relationships, research notes, action outcomes, an agenda, longitudinal continuity runs, on-demand contextual recall and provenance-aware reference material. Tool-generated research activity can update the operational record, subject to the system's authority policy.

## Experimental value and limit

The agent can supply behavioral *reference traces* for comparison, but opinions of congruence and fluent research collaboration are not ground truth about causal neural identity. Any comparisons to the experimental subject require blinded scoring, information-parity accounting, model/version recording, and a clear distinction between externally retrieved identity and learned substrate features.

This is a distinct reference condition, not a fourth production Pretorius. There is no independent generalized continuity performance claim established by this record.
