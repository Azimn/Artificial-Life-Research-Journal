# Champion versus Challenger

## Core research question

Which proven non-LLM mechanisms actually improve believable persistent characters when compared under controlled conditions?

## Methodological stance

Existing implementations are evidence sources.

New mechanisms are hypotheses.

The current champion remains frozen while challengers are tested under identical scenarios and seeds.

Promotion depends on predefined criteria rather than architectural novelty.

## Target phenotype

The desired character should exhibit persistent choices, routines, relationships, avoidance, preferences, memory, goals, mistakes, adaptation, conflicts, habits, spontaneous activity, social expectations, and accumulated consequences.

Natural-language generation is optional and must not be the source of the apparent mind.

## Relationship to PEMA

PEMA emerged within this research environment as a deeper test of whether local asymmetry, scarce processing, and persistent concerns can produce behavioral individuality.
