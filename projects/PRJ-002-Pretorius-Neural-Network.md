# Pretorius Neural Network

## Core research question

Can a character phenotype become a property of a plastic recurrent neural substrate rather than a prompt, lookup table, or trained output decoder?

## Construction routes

Biography development begins with a generic network and exposes it to chronological developmental history.

Persona Inverse Synthesis begins with the same generic architecture and trains from mature behavioral constraints.

A long-term question is whether the two routes converge on similar behavior and causal neural organization.

## Key results so far

Identity Surgery showed that most measurable v0.3 inverse-synthesized phenotype followed the learned motor decoder.

Decoder Amputation showed that default recurrent plasticity did not expose a strong phenotype directly from neural action populations.

Biography Surgery produced the same qualitative localization and exposed a neural-update budget confound.

The Recurrent Embedding Sweep produced a reproducible recurrent-only phenotype signal under stronger but bounded plasticity, while excessive plasticity degraded discrimination.

## Current direction

The v0.4 line makes recurrent-only performance primary and uses lesions, grafts, transplants, functional-core tests, and convergence tests before opening sealed terminal evaluation.

## BioCircuit experimental extension, 2026-10-08

BioCircuit is an experimental `biocircuit/` subpackage of the [same neural network repository](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network), not a competing Pretorius product. BC00 tested a 4,096-unit matched synthetic compartment-local versus global inhibitory architecture on random cue associations. Local competition achieved high trained-cue accuracy but worse damaged-cue robustness than the simpler global comparator.

BC01 uses the frozen 450-memory Pretorius autobiography through either separate original lexical hashing or pinned shared L2 v2 deterministic lexical cache and a BioCircuit-specific 256-channel adapter. D1 and D2 did not establish source-conditioned neural action learning; tenfold plasticity collapsed choices to a single class. D3 showed 20.14% on reused development cues, equal to controls. D4 gain sweeps and D5 eligibility diagnostics isolated weak, source-nonselective recurrent action influence. D5B's decoder-only 100% on reused labelled cards was in-sample.

[BC01-D6A](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Neural-Network/blob/main/results/biocircuit/BC01_D6A_RESULTS.md) withheld supervised labels and measured only 18.75% correct on original held-out decision probes, 20.14% on unreviewed candidate paraphrases, compared with 25% balanced four-class chance. New recurrent W and its exact lesion behaved identically on these tasks. These are *transductive* and not independent episode/source holdouts. No BioCircuit neural mechanism is approved for automatic import into The Doctor Lives.
