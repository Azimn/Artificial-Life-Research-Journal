# NPC Steering Plus: Closed-Loop Activation-Space Affect

## Research question

Can compact persistent symbolic affect drive a frozen language model's expressive behavior through activation steering, with the resulting expressed state fed back into the controller, rather than storing personality in the model's trained weights?

## Demonstration and experiments

[npc-steering-plus](https://github.com/Azimn/npc-steering-plus) uses a three-driver, shared-event scenario and a symbolic affect controller coupled to activation directions in a frozen Qwen-family model. The README documents generation of per-event traces, validation of valence/arousal/dominance probes, calibration attempts, arousal-gate ablation, a card-versus-steering 2x2 attribution, and cross-modal audio auditing. The precise numeric findings and negative decay-calibration results must be read from the project's analysis documents rather than inferred from sample dialogue.

The separate [Bride donor table](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Bride-of-Frankenstein/blob/main/README.md) identifies activation-space steering as **blocked** for transfer until compatible live-model reproduction. That is a qualification status, not a conclusion that activation steering is ineffective.

## Scientific boundary

Distinct generated monologues are demonstrations, not proof of durable personality, generalization, or history-dependent learning. Separate symbolic affect effects, prompt-card effects, model-specific steering/probe quality, latency, and persistent carryover. Run ablations on fixed events and renderer substitutions before proposing any production donor.

## Position in the portfolio

This is a separate expression/control mechanism that can be compared against external history and recurring neural state, but it is not the fly connectome and is not a validated autonomous organism.
