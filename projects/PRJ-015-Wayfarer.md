# Wayfarer

Wayfarer tested the idea that one artificial subject should remain continuous while its renderer, model, device, interface, or embodiment changes.

Its core research question was not whether a persona prompt could be copied between systems, but whether identity could live in a model-independent persistent substrate while different language models and front ends acted as interchangeable expression organs.

Wayfarer therefore belongs in the portable-identity lineage between P3, Persona Engine, and later renderer-independent organism architectures.

The project helped sharpen the distinction between canonical subject state and the model currently expressing that state.
