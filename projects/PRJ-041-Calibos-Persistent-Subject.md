# Calibos Mind: Endogenous Subject and Longitudinal Practice

## Research question

Can an independently continuing subject use bounded first-person memory, attention, unresolved concerns, thought provenance, dreams and offline consolidation to acquire enduring behavioral history outside isolated chats?

## Implementation

[Calibos Mind](https://github.com/Azimn/calibos-mind) documents a persistent endogenous workspace based on a frozen Jelly-Psiduck v0.2 engine snapshot. It has queued prompts, private thought, silence, salience, concern closure, consolidation proposals, dream fragments and provenance-aware contact observations. The repository explicitly says it is **not** a frozen scientific harness with sealed cases. Its use is observational and subject-specific.

The engine version, privacy, persistent state, and operational history matter for interpreting later behavior. The public journal should describe architectural mechanisms and public code only. Do not copy private thought streams, inbox material, personal correspondence, private backup details, private state databases or inferred relationships into this record.

## Scientific role

Calibos is an operational case study and potential structural donor, not a confirmed validation of externalized identity. Naturalistic coordination such as [Game Night](https://github.com/Azimn/game-night) is useful for designing later social-continuity benchmarks, but scripted or unblinded successes must not become causal evidence. Preserving the separation from Kiki and Pretorius is essential.

## Follow-up

Define an opt-in, privacy-safe blinded continuity evaluation and public trace schema before deriving statistics or independent claims. Do not turn private subjective reports into inferred consciousness evidence.
