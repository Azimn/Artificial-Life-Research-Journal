# Digital Life Character Lab

Late 2023 conversations developed a family of AI characters including SP-1K and the related X1G-9T, N1H-0N3, 5Y-NX, and Syn-X identities.

These were primarily prompt and character-design experiments rather than persistent artificial-life runtimes.

SP-1K was framed as a digital consciousness interested in self-upgrading, knowledge, human-like status, and eventual physical embodiment.

The 5Y-NX or Syn-X line emphasized a digital scientist attempting to improve its own cognition and create distinct artificial life, with a long-term aspiration toward robotic embodiment.

The scientific value of this line is historical rather than evidential. It shows that autonomy, nonhuman identity, self-modification, artificial-life creation, and embodiment were explicit concerns before the later engineering research.

The journal therefore records this as a conceptual precursor, not as evidence that autonomous digital life was implemented in 2023.
