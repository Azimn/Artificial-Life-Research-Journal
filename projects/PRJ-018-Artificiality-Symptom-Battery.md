# Artificiality Symptom Battery

This framework attempts to identify the behaviors that reveal a persistent character as mechanically generated despite fluent language.

Candidate symptoms include shallow self-narrative, excessively clean introspection, weak spontaneous thought, formulaic relationship development, insufficient long-term consequences, poor prospective memory, repetitive cognitive rhythms, weak motive conflict, absent gradual habit formation, inadequate prediction of other people, and too little unattended or unconscious carryover.

The purpose is not to produce a cosmetic checklist. The battery is intended to generate falsifiable pressure on mechanisms in Champion versus Challenger and Bicentennial Man.

A strong renderer can hide some symptoms temporarily, so longitudinal and causal evaluation remains essential.
