# Developmental and Cognitive Theory Notebook

This record preserves recurring hypotheses that have not yet become independent experimental programs.

Topics include empathy as a predictive model of others, meta-stability and rollback for self-modification, multi-objective value learning, developmental social interaction, internal multi-agent negotiation, affect-intelligence coherence, simulation as an experimental cradle, compression-first intelligence, cognitive phase transitions, synthetic curiosity, error signatures of intelligence, and multi-agent identity formation.

The purpose of this notebook is to prevent promising theoretical ideas from disappearing while also avoiding premature promotion into full projects.

When one of these hypotheses receives a clear falsifiable design and sustained experimental lineage, it should be split into its own project or experiment record.
