# Kurzweil Brain Experiments

## Core research question

Can the same artificial newborn become systematically different adults when raised in different information-rich developmental environments?

A narrower experiment asks whether one reconstructed life moves the organism toward a held-out behavioral phenotype associated with a known person.

## Experimental firewall

Developmental inputs contain situations, generic actions, and action-contingent consequences.

They do not contain the target adult answer key or historical action labels.

Adult phenotype evaluation is held out from development.

## v0.1 result

The canonical Kurzweil-derived condition exceeded the matched-rich creative-humanities control in 11 of 12 paired lives.

However, the computer-access ablation exceeded canonical in all 12 paired lives.

## Interpretation

The result supports developmental differentiation in the simulator but does not establish a Kurzweil-specific developmental attractor.

The failed ablation exposed an architectural problem: broad reinforcement of creation behavior appears to be overgeneralized rather than sufficiently context-dependent.

## Current direction

Context-conditioned action learning and reward-matched ablations are the appropriate next tests while preserving the founder and paired-seed structure.
