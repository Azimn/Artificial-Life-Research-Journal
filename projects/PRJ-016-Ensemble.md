# Ensemble

Ensemble explored whether multiple internal candidate processes could generate competing interpretations, intentions, or responses while a separate authority mechanism determined what became canonical action or speech.

The research question was whether internal plurality could improve realism and cognitive richness without allowing every generated thought to become authoritative state.

This project is conceptually related to the 2023 Modular Cognitive Agents work, later Ensemble-style proposal competition, DUCK motive competition, and PEMA's content-blind allocation.

Its key contribution is the separation of proposal generation from authority over the subject.
