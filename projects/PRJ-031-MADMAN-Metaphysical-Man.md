# MADMAN / Metaphysical Man

Metaphysical Man became the home for MADMAN, Metaphysical Artificial Dynamics for Metabolic Autopoiesis and Noesis, in September 2026.

The experiment explored whether local resource dynamics, persistent structural scars, reconstruction, competition, and distributed process interaction could generate organized behavior without beginning from conventional memory, attention, personality, or self modules.

A hostile review concluded that the strongest evidence supported weak emergence only. The system showed history-dependent distributed dynamics and resource competition, but it had not demonstrated memory, subjectivity, introspection, personality, or autopoiesis in the stronger senses those terms can imply.

This result should be preserved as a useful boundary finding. MADMAN tests how far bottom-up organization can go before familiar cognitive mechanisms must be added or independently demonstrated.
