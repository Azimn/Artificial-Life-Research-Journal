# Personal Cognitive Digital Twin / JayTwin

This line began as a portable cognitive and behavioral twin rather than a fictional-character system.

The central question was whether a system could reproduce a particular person's reasoning patterns, autobiographical knowledge, decision tendencies, and expressive behavior across both frontier models and smaller local models.

The March design combined psychometric measures, cognitive-style tasks, autobiographical interviews, scenario responses, natural-language samples, structured persona data, retrieval, and self-consistency evaluation.

By June, JayTwin had become a runnable prototype with local retrieval, graph invariants and contradictions, corrections, a silent ledger, an internal fidelity critic, and multiple model backends.

The project is related to Persona Engine but should not be collapsed into it. Persona Engine asks how a persistent artificial character can remain itself. JayTwin adds the harder constraint of fidelity to a specific human source.
