# Gelatinblob

## Core research question

Can recurrent adaptive state provide persistent nonlinguistic carryover and learning beneath a semantic persona system?

## Mechanism family

Recurrent adaptive state.

Hebbian or Oja-like plasticity.

Metaplasticity.

Prediction error.

Provisional synaptic tagging.

Outcome-dependent consolidation.

Replay.

Checkpoints and rollback.

## Experimental role

Gelatinblob occupies an important position between explicit persona state and fully substrate-first artificial life.

It treats adaptive dynamics as a sidecar whose learned internal organization can influence the subject without becoming an omniscient semantic authority.

## Open need

The historical blind comparisons and later v0.10 to v0.11 experiments should be backfilled from preserved chats and artifacts so the result history is not reduced to architectural memory.
