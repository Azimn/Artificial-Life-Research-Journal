# Portable Identity Standards Line

This record preserves several closely related predecessor systems: Open Persona Specification, SoulCore Framework, Project Persona, OmniPersona Core, Persistent Persona Platform or P3, Persona Framework v3, the Three-Tier Digital Companion work, and related cartridge formats.

The shared question was what minimum portable informational structure is sufficient to preserve a synthetic individual's identity, memory, relationships, behavioral constraints, and continuity across sessions, models, front ends, and hardware.

SoulCore Framework v3.0 emphasized persistent identity, a Voice Gate, session snapshots, self-consistency, memory, and bounded self-reference.

Project Persona, OmniPersona, and P3 moved toward stable SNP identity snapshots plus mutable SOL continuity or life-log state, with memory and relationships external to the model.

This lineage directly precedes PersonaConsole and Persona Engine. It deserves its own record because the shift from prompting to portable identity substrate was a major conceptual transition rather than a mere file-format change.
