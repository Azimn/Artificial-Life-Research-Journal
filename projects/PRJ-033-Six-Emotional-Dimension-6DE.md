# Six Emotional Dimension Model

The Six Emotional Dimension model was developed in 2023 as a text-oriented framework for representing nuanced emotional state.

The six dimensions were arousal, valence, dominance, agency, fidelity, and novelty.

The model was intended to infer subtle or indirect emotional cues from text and guide more context-sensitive artificial responses. The goal was not psychoanalytic diagnosis, but richer emotionally appropriate behavior.

Later conversations identify the work as published in 2023.

The audit did not recover evidence of a validated standalone 6DE software implementation from that period. However, the model clearly influenced later companion and persona designs, where persistent emotional state became a first-class component.

Later systems often simplified or substituted affect representations such as PAD-style variables, but the core research question remained: can a compact explicit affect state provide useful continuity and behavioral modulation beyond language-model improvisation?
