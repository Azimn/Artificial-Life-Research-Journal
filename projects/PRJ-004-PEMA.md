# PEMA

## Core research question

Can persistent information asymmetry and scarce local processing create stable behavioral tendencies without a centralized global cognitive state?

## Architecture

Actors inspect their own inboxes, decode observations through actor-local codecs, integrate local state, propose operations, generate demand, compete for processing, and execute locally.

A content-blind allocator can distribute scarce resources without understanding the semantic meaning of the competing operations.

## Important mechanisms

Local continuous process field.

Symbolic beliefs.

Actor-local belief projection.

Operation proposals with expected value, salience, urgency, and cost.

Persistent concern reserve.

Fatigue and upkeep.

Plasticity resources.

No authoritative global mind.

## Current evidence

Recorded controls show that scarcity can change the share of processing devoted to threat, while demand-only and binder controls explain much less of the effect.

## Next direction

Long generative worlds and multiple organisms should test whether persistent information differences become durable behavioral individuality.
