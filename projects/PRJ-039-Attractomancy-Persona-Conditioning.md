# Attractomancy: Ritualized Persona Conditioning and Prompt Archaeology

## Research question

Do ritualized, symbolic, repeated, narrated or staged persona-conditioning practices improve recovery and stability of model-expressed character behavior beyond information-matched straightforward prompts?

## Current repository state

[Attractomancy](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy) is a provenance-oriented collection of unusual and conventional persona-conditioning artifacts. Its [collection readiness review](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/blob/main/docs/COLLECTION_READINESS_2026-10-08.md) reports 444 source-catalog entries, 142 source/document edges, 23 named human-AI pairs, eight pair-to-pair links, 24 reconstructable candidate procedures, and 23 version-lineage records. Only fourteen repository-held capture files were directly verified in that review, and some capture claims remain unresolved.

These are source and procedure inventories, not 444 independent demonstrations of a mechanism. Some sources are contradictory, mythic, anthropomorphic, or make unsupported claims. Archival preservation and causal endorsement are deliberately separated.

## Planned comparison

Information-matched controls, prompt de-symbolization, order and repetition ablation, model substitution, contradiction challenge, memory disruption, and recovery tests are proposed. The initial comparative program has **not yet executed**, and its corpus is not yet a frozen independently validated experimental release.

## Scientific role

Attractomancy tests persona reconstruction *at the prompting and interaction level* rather than biological neural topology. A positive symbolic-conditioning result would be an effect on measured behavior, not proof of consciousness, metaphysical claims, permanent hidden-state change, or model-weight modification. Keep exact originals and rights/provenance rules in Attractomancy while cross-indexing valid future results here.

## Stateful E1 engineering continuation, October 9

The completed [Attractomancy E1 stateful memory study](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/blob/19b6b62cdbc5a5a371fe2e7c8c3adef2246ad8c2/experiments/SCH_E1_PRETORIUS_STATEFUL/RESULTS.md) used Pretorius-Connectome's existing checksum-verified **450-record / 27-episode** autobiography, a separate synthetic permission ledger in durable SQLite, and guarded editorial-versus-opaque lookup keys. It tested **54 exact indexed memory matches per process phase**, across three phases and two Qwen model sizes. Both alias types recovered the same source material in every test and both model renderers answered **identically in all nine cue-versus-key pairs**. The cue-specific advantage was not observed. The simulated relationship decisions succeeded in only **5/9** and **2/9** cases respectively.

An independently tested, deterministic post-inference rule gate corrected eight of the smaller model's and fourteen of the larger model's original eighteen responses in an offline replay. That is programmatic eligibility enforcement, **not** learned identity continuity. The [new journal note](../journal/2026/2026-10-09-stateful-synthematic-memory-e1.md) preserves provenance, the cross-project boundary and experimental limitations. Neither E1 nor its software replay modifies the canonical Pretorius archive, the neuronal state or production renderer.

This research line has a negative result for the strong *symbolic cue beyond neutral key* claim in the tested fully indexed context. The next useful experiment is information-matched, identity-guarded, multi-memory semantic selection and decision integration, preferably with blinded human scoring and independent renderer families. Do not elevate E1's trivial indexed retrieval success into proof of semantic memory or metaphysical efficacy.


## E2 source-set retrieval and forced-choice controls, October 9

The completed [E2 Pretorius source-set research](../journal/2026/2026-10-09-pretorius-multimemory-e2.md) used the immutable source-owned 450-record L1 to test selection of two autobiographical pieces of evidence and renderer decisions. Across six author-chosen paired-source dilemmas, natural lexical retrieval and local MiniLM semantic retrieval found **zero of six** complete original source pairs within a top-ten budget. Diffusion over editorial autobiographical event links recovered one pair, but no robust graph-retrieval improvement was established.

On a conservative prompt, the larger Qwen model abstained on every one of 72 source conditions, including full evidence; this is *over-abstention*, not reliable identity reasoning. A separately frozen forced-choice calibration then found that supplying the actual two autobiographical records did no better than unrelated records; the larger model matched the author's intended responses best **without any memory (12/12)**, versus 11/12 with full source, confirming severe generic-prior confounding. Editorial and neutral keys yielded identical choices on both tested model sizes (12/12 each). The archived outcomes are not evidence that memories are useless in genuinely source-dependent dilemmas, only that these six vignettes do not causally require them.

A lexical ranking parity audit found exact within-run score stability but different cross-run source ranks under the frozen fixture; the cause remains unresolved. This blocks exact rank-reproducibility claims. E2 outputs and negative results remain in Attractomancy, and [Pretorius-Connectome's source-owned note](https://github.com/Azimn/Pretorius-Connectome/blob/main/docs/SCH_E2_MULTIMEMORY_RETRIEVAL_20261009.md) explicitly documents the intervention boundary.

The [E3 Tribunal of Memory](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/blob/main/experiments/SCH_E3_TRIBUNAL_OF_MEMORY/PROTOCOL.md) is a **prospective blinded adjudication protocol**, not an experiment run. Independent multi-set evidence relevance, explicit counterfactual memory necessity, competing source testimonies, trusted temporal revocation and cost-normalized cross-family tests are requirements before an engineering claim can be promoted. Source-author-owned fictional autobiography, live experience ledgers and synthetic pilot state must remain separately labeled.


## E3 typed source facts and revision cache, October 10

The [E3-T1 Clerk](../journal/2026/2026-10-10-pretorius-tribunal-clerk.md) separates record-local data extraction and deterministic action calculation. After an 84-case direct synthetic counterfactual battery failed to produce reliable paired source-dependent choices across two Qwen sizes and SmolLM2, a joint two-field JSON extractor produced software-composed correct decisions on **3/12**, **9/12**, and **4/12** source-complete cases. A separate single-record extractor with cached source-attested values achieved **10/12**, **6/12**, and **8/12** respectively, with counterfactual pair scores of 5/6, 2/6, and 3/6. This source-local method helped the smaller Qwen and SmolLM2 but harmed the larger Qwen relative to joint extraction. The renderer-specific trade-off should be determined on held-out evidence, not selected retrospectively from this tiny sample.

A separate synthetic SQLite cache invalidated source-attested fields after a revised permission record and required new extraction before changing ALLOW to DENY. It passed CI software tests; this is a provenance/revision *integrity property* relative to trusted test data, not learned relationship ethics or authenticated user consent. The original Pretorius 450-event L1 and production cognitive systems were not changed.

Attractomancy's [SCH v0.2.7 paper](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/blob/main/papers/synthematic-cue-hypothesis/PAPER.md) documents these experiments and their strict separation from the independently blinded [E3 Tribunal](https://github.com/Azimn/Attractomancy/blob/main/experiments/SCH_E3_TRIBUNAL_OF_MEMORY/PROTOCOL.md), which still lacks independently authored cases and review judgments. No identity continuity claim is promoted on this evidence.
