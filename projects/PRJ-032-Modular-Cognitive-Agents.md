# Modular Cognitive Agents / Synthesizing Sentience

This 2023 research line explored whether human-like cognitive complexity could be approximated by coordinating specialized autonomous or language-model-based agents representing different psychological functions.

The architecture proposed modular mental components, smaller specialist models, an executive or self-preserving coordinating layer, memory, emotional processing, and eventual embodiment.

The deeper question was whether a coherent continuing self could arise from coordinated specialized processes rather than one monolithic model.

Later conversations identify this work as published in 2023. The audit did not recover evidence of a completed software prototype from that period.

Historically, this is a direct intellectual ancestor of later projects such as Sidecar Self-Core, Ensemble, DUCK, and the broader Cognitive Organs idea. The later work differs mainly in becoming more explicit about canonical state, first-person access, causal authority, non-LLM mechanisms, and falsifiable tests.
