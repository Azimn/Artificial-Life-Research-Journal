# Frankenstein and Bride: Causal Character Runtime and Donor Qualification

## Research question

Can a renderer-neutral persistent character runtime demonstrate history-sensitive and prospective behavior without giving generated text authority over memory or world truth, and can a frozen baseline objectively qualify improvements from older research donors?

## Implementations and preserved results

[Frankenstein v0.1](https://github.com/Azimn/Frankenstein/blob/main/README.md) is a deterministic, local-first system with event ledger, replayable projections, belief and world-authority boundaries, memory, relationships, concerns, needs, habits, commitments, and a single action selector. Its repository reports 51 deterministic tests and 15 acceptance probes, with a corrected nine-job Windows/macOS/Linux matrix. These are engineering and causal-harness findings, not blinded evidence of subjective identity.

[The Bride of Frankenstein](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Bride-of-Frankenstein/blob/main/README.md) is a distinct *qualification phase in the same lineage*, rather than a new standalone mind. It freezes v0.1 as the champion, tests candidate donor mechanisms against a real runtime, and records both promotions and rejections. Its documented v0.2 candidate includes bounded planning, constrained inner-ear concern feedback, narrow confidentiality commitment pressure, and an involuntary-expression channel. The first-person perception bridge remains an adapter. README-reported longitudinal and stress qualifications run nine integrated trials and eight additional 32-turn trials with cross-platform software checks.

## Important negative and held results

Selective recall and Digital Subject continuity influence were redundant under the frozen Bride targets. Offscreen catch-up improved a targeted behavior but failed its cost gate. Contextual recurrent/plastic policy improved an authored behavior but failed the complexity gate. Activation steering remained blocked pending suitable live-model reproduction. These are *target-specific donor decisions*, not refutations of the donors' scientific programs.

## Scientific role

This is the strongest existing implementation of explicit champion/challenger rules in the portfolio. Reuse its donor classification, source provenance, exact runtime comparison and negative-result discipline under PRJ-013 and the [continuity program](../programs/CHARACTER_CONTINUITY_PROGRAM_V1.md). Product certification is not independent scientific validation.

## Authority and boundary

The Frankenstein and Bride repositories own their original frozen evidence, tests, promotion decisions, release notes and provenance. Do not confuse this runtime with the unrelated Frankenstein Village MUD. Keep The Doctor Lives as a separate definitive Pretorius character rather than silently replacing it.
