# Digital Subject / Digital Organism

This line explored the transition from a persistent persona into a continuously running artificial subject.

The central question was whether continuity could arise from an autonomous simulation with state, memory, needs, action, consequence, and ongoing change rather than from isolated conversations.

The project emphasized that the subject should continue to exist between conversational turns, that internal state should evolve independently of user prompting, and that the language model should function as an optional interface rather than the entire organism.

This line is a direct predecessor to DUCK, Subjective Character Loop, and later same-origin developmental experiments.
