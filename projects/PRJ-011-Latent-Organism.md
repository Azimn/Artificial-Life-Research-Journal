# Latent Organism

## Core research question

Can a pretrained transformer's representational geometry serve as a persistent developmental substrate when organism state is retained externally and repeatedly reinjected?

## Distinction

Latent Organism is not the portfolio's first substrate experiment.

It is the transformer-representation member of a broader lineage that already includes artificial chemistry, native computational physics, recurrent neural substrates, graph topology, and adaptive recurrent sidecars.

## Hypothesis

Repeated developmental experience may drive the system toward a characteristic representational basin that influences future processing even when the renderer is treated as a replaceable organ.

## Strong tests

Same-origin divergence.

History retention.

Model swap robustness.

Lesion or state-removal sensitivity.

Attractor formation.

Transfer between compatible renderers.

Comparison against semantically equivalent but developmentally different state.
