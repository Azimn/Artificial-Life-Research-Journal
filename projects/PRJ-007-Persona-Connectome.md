# Persona Connectome

## Core research question

Can individual identity be carried by persistent typed topology plus dynamic activation and history rather than by a prompt or flat personality specification?

## Key mechanisms

Persistent persona topology.

Recurrent activation.

Inhibition.

Neuromodulatory or global dynamic influence.

Historical-memory recruitment.

Perturbation and recovery.

## Relationship to DUCK

The connectome line motivated the idea that character-specific organization can be sealed as a structural influence while autobiographical memory, relationships, beliefs, commitments, and learning remain properties of the living organism.

## Open question

The project needs systematic destructive tests establishing which topology is necessary, sufficient, transferable, or developmentally plastic.
