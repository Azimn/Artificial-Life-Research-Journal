# Subjective Character Loop

## Core research question

Can a character operate entirely through first-person natural-language experience while hidden numerical and implementation state remains inaccessible?

## v0.1

The prototype separates private thought from intentional speech.

Hidden hunger, fatigue, pain, temperature discomfort, boredom, and interest can trigger first-person awareness without revealing the values that generated it.

Sudden pain can also produce an involuntary vocalization path.

## v0.2 direction

Continuation from previous subjective state was tested as a mechanism for stronger experiential continuity.

Matched-history testing produced different motive-level outcomes after supportive versus adverse histories, indicating that the loop could carry more than independent prompt-local narration.

## Boundary

The loop is not itself a full world simulation. It isolates subjective access before larger perception, relationship, and cognitive systems are added.
