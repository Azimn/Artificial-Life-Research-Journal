# Bicentennial Man

## Core research question

Given an already persistent artificial organism, what bounded changes produce behavior that remains less detectably artificial across histories and unfamiliar situations?

## Founder

MicroPsiDUCK v0.10 is the first founder organism.

## Experimental sequence

Founder phenotype characterization.

Accelerated Life experience-only divergence.

Matched-history determinism controls.

Surface-Mutable Control.

Evaluator exploit analysis.

Fixed-boundary Mechanism-Mutable DUCK.

Held-out transfer.

Ablation.

## Key methodological principle

A higher training score is not treated as cognitive improvement until the change survives fixed-boundary evaluation and held-out transfer.

## Importance

Bicentennial Man turns earlier architecture work into an experimental evolution and improvement laboratory. It also supplies a framework for distinguishing genuine mechanism change from surface-level evaluator exploitation.
