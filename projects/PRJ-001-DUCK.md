# DUCK / MicroPsiDUCK

## Core research question

Can a believable continuing artificial subject be implemented as a persistent causal organism whose present cognition depends on limited first-person access, lived history, motivation, memory, relationships, commitments, action, consequence, learning, recovery, and planning?

## Architectural stance

The renderer is optional and non-authoritative.

The organism distinguishes needs, motives, goals, plans, intentions, actions, outcomes, and learning.

Implementation telemetry can drive the system without being exposed to the subject.

## Major experimental sequence

Subjective Moment and access firewall.

Regulation and recovery.

Longitudinal life simulation.

Prospective agency and persistent intentions.

Endogenous goal formation and replanning.

MicroPsi-inspired motivated cognition, associative activation, and global modulators.

Language-lesion testing.

Accelerated Life through Bicentennial Man.

## Current role

DUCK is the most complete cognitive-organism line in the portfolio and increasingly serves as an experimental subject rather than as a final architecture.
