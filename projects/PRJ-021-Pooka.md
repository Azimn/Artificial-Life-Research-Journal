# Pooka

Pooka is one of the earliest recovered projects in the research lineage, with documented discussion by April 2023.

Its original form was an AI educational companion, cohort, friend, tutor, and pet for children. The concept included adaptive interaction, web API integration with Unity or Unreal, app and web access, egg-hatching and interaction-driven customization, and a future path toward a physical toy.

The core question was already recognizable: can an AI companion become more than a session chatbot by combining conversation with persistent state, personalization, memory, simulation, and game-like presence?

A related 2024 child-friendly chatbot prototype explored small local models including TinyLlama, Phi-2, and small Llama variants, along with working, middle, and long-term memory, retention of child-specific details, and six-dimensional emotional tracking. The retrieved historical evidence indicates that a chat interface existed and that TinyLlama generation problems were encountered, but does not establish a completed Pooka product.

Pooka should not be retroactively described as having the later DUCK, PEMA, or Persona Engine architecture. Its significance is historical. It places the persistent-companion problem at least as early as 2023 and shows that memory, emotional continuity, customization, portability, and possible embodiment were present well before the 2025 portable-identity standards work.
