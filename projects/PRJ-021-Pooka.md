# Pooka

Pooka is the earliest clearly recovered 2026 project in this audit.

Its core question was whether an LLM-based children's digital companion could become more than a chatbot by combining conversation with simulation, persistent state, memory, and virtual-pet mechanics.

The project is important historically because it already joined several ideas that later became separate research programs: persistent identity, autonomous state change, relationship continuity, embodied or game-like presence, and the distinction between a conversational interface and a continuing simulated entity.

Pooka should not be retroactively described as having the later DUCK or PEMA architecture. Its significance is developmental. It shows that the research program began 2026 with the problem of how to make a digital companion continue to exist as something rather than simply answer prompts.
