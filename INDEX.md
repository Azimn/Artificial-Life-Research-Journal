## Latest cumulative evidence: Vector Fly → Definitive Pretorius

[Vector Fly real retrieval and dialogue A/B journal entry](journal/2026/2026-10-08-vector-fly-to-definitive-pretorius.md) connects the 450-memory pinned lexical database/API, live source-verified production brain, and [twelve real A/B replies](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/main/results/vector_fly/stage02-run37873205792/DIALOGUE_RAW_AB.json). External memory changed four of six small-model replies but did not demonstrate accuracy or identity improvement; hallucinated details, chronology mixing and two nonanswers were preserved. The [masked human scoring method](https://github.com/Azimn/The-Doctor-Lives/blob/main/docs/VECTOR_FLY_BLINDED_REVIEW_STAGE03.md) is implemented but no independently adjudicated scores exist. This is a distinct *renderer effects* test, not evidence for biological synaptic storage.
